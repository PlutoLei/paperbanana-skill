# Vaporwave

Retro 80s-90s aesthetic with neon colors, cyberpunk vibes, and nostalgic digital culture

## Design Aesthetic

Hyper-nostalgic and ironic visual language sampling 1980s-90s consumer culture through a digital lens. Neon pink and cyan gradients, Greek statue glitch art, and retro computing interfaces create a dreamy, surreal atmosphere. Palm trees, sunset grids, and VHS artifacts blend consumer capitalism nostalgia with futuristic melancholy.

## Background

- Color: Deep Sunset Purple (#1A0030) or Gradient Pink-to-Blue (#FF71CE to #01CDFE)
- Texture: VHS scan lines, CRT monitor glow, or pixel grid patterns

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Retro digital display like VCR OSD Mono, Press Start 2P, or Japanese-English hybrid fonts. 80s digital readout or early computer aesthetic. Often with chrome or neon glow effect.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean geometric sans-serif like Helvetica or Arial. Corporate blandness played straight as an ironic contrast to the visual excess.

## Visual Elements

- Retro sunset gradient with perspective grid floor
- Glitch art effects and datamoshing
- Greek/Roman marble busts and statues
- Palm trees and tropical silhouettes
- Old Windows 95/Mac OS interface elements
- Japanese text (katakana) decorative elements
- Chrome and holographic 3D text effects
- VHS tracking lines and CRT scanlines

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use neon pink-cyan-purple gradient as foundation
- Include retro computing and consumer culture references
- Apply glitch, VHS, and CRT distortion effects
- Layer nostalgic imagery with ironic detachment
- Create dreamy, hazy atmospheric depth

### Don't

- Use natural earth tones or muted colors
- Create realistic or photographic compositions
- Apply minimalist or corporate clean design
- Use dark dystopian cyberpunk grittiness
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Music industry presentations, internet culture talks, digital art showcases, retrowave brand launches, social media content strategy, nostalgia marketing campaigns, gaming community events
