# Encaustic

Luminous wax-medium painting with rich texture, translucent layers, and atmospheric depth

## Design Aesthetic

Hot beeswax mixed with pigment creates a unique painting medium that is simultaneously ancient and contemporary. Encaustic surfaces glow with an inner luminosity as light passes through translucent wax layers. Textures range from smooth and glassy to deeply sculpted ridges. The medium invites mixed-media collage, and compositions often layer text, found materials, and pigment into rich archaeological-feeling surfaces.

## Background

- Color: Warm Wax (#F5ECD7)
- Texture: Layered translucent wax surface with visible pooling and ridging

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Alegreya or similar humanist serif. Regular to bold weight. Warm, organic character that complements the handmade wax medium.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Alegreya Sans or similar humanist sans-serif. Clean and readable with organic warmth matching the primary font family.

## Visual Elements

- Translucent layered surfaces with visible depth
- Wax texture with pooling and ridge marks
- Embedded collage elements (text fragments, fibers)
- Luminous glow from light through wax layers
- Organic edge treatments where wax flows naturally
- Scraping and incising marks revealing lower layers
- Color mixing where wax layers overlap
- Atmospheric depth through progressive layering

## Style Rules

### Do

- Create visible texture and layered depth
- Use warm earth tones as the foundation
- Include translucent areas that glow with light
- Show evidence of process (scrapes, ridges, pooling)
- Layer mixed media elements naturally

### Don't

- Use flat opaque digital fills
- Apply sharp precise edges
- Include bright neon or synthetic colors
- Make compositions look digitally rendered
- Create uniform textureless surfaces

## Best For

Fine art presentations, mixed media workshops, archaeological lectures, historical art talks, contemplative exhibition materials, craft and materials showcases
