# Nautical

Maritime-themed design with ocean blues, naval elements, and coastal atmosphere

## Design Aesthetic

Fresh and adventurous compositions inspired by the open sea, maritime navigation, and coastal life. Navy blues, crisp whites, and weathered textures evoke sailing charts, harbor towns, and ocean voyages. The mood balances rugged authenticity with clean, navigational precision.

## Background

- Color: Sea Foam White (#F0F8FF)
- Texture: Weathered canvas or nautical chart paper with faint compass rose watermark

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Oswald or similar bold condensed sans-serif. Strong, commanding letterforms reminiscent of ship hull lettering. Confident and directional.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Lora or similar classic serif for body text. Maritime log-book quality. Warm readability with traditional character.

## Visual Elements

- Compass rose and navigation instrument motifs
- Anchor, helm wheel, and rope knot decorations
- Wave patterns and tide line dividers
- Nautical flag semaphore accents
- Map-style grid overlays and coordinate markers
- Ship silhouettes and lighthouse icons
- Rope-style borders and knot corner pieces
- Weathered wood and canvas textures

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use navy and ocean blue as dominant palette anchors
- Incorporate maritime iconography naturally
- Maintain a sense of open space and horizon
- Use weathered textures for authentic feel
- Keep layouts structured like navigational charts

### Don't

- Use landlocked imagery (desert, mountain, urban)
- Include rustic or woodland themes
- Apply dark, claustrophobic compositions
- Use neon or overly modern color treatments
- Clutter the layout with excessive detail

## Best For

Travel and tourism presentations, marine biology talks, shipping and logistics overviews, coastal brand storytelling, adventure and exploration themes, naval history
