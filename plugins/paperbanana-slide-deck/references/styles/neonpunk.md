# Neonpunk

Cyberpunk neon-drenched aesthetic with magenta highlights, dark purple shadows, and ultramodern edge

## Design Aesthetic

A high-voltage fusion of cyberpunk and vaporwave visual culture. Neon-saturated colors blaze against deep purple and black backgrounds. Magenta highlights clash with teal shadows in dramatic high-contrast compositions. The style is sleek, ultramodern, and stunningly vibrant, channeling the electric energy of rain-slicked neon cityscapes and cutting-edge digital interfaces.

## Background

- Color: Deep Purple-Black (#0D0018) or Dark Cyber (#12001E)
- Texture: Subtle scan-line artifacts with distant neon glow reflections

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Ultra-modern condensed or extended sans-serif with sharp, angular letterforms. Often rendered with neon glow or chrome effect. Bold, sleek, and futuristic.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean monospace or geometric sans-serif. Crisp, technical, readable against dark vibrant backgrounds. Often with subtle neon underlining.

## Visual Elements

- Neon light tubes and glowing edge outlines
- Rain reflections and wet surface neon bounces
- Holographic interface elements and HUD overlays
- Cyberpunk cityscape silhouettes in the background
- Chrome and metallic surface textures
- Scan-line and CRT monitor artifacts
- High-contrast lighting with dark purple shadow areas
- Geometric grid patterns receding into perspective

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use intensely saturated neon colors against deep dark backgrounds
- Create high contrast between glowing elements and shadows
- Maintain sleek, ultramodern, professional quality
- Apply neon glow effects on edges and key elements
- Keep compositions detailed, crisp, and visually stunning

### Don't

- Use painterly, illustrative, or hand-drawn styles
- Apply muted, desaturated, or pastel colors
- Create glitchy, deformed, or ugly distortions
- Use dull, flat lighting without neon energy
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos
