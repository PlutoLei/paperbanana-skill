# Steampunk

Victorian-industrial aesthetic with brass, copper, gears, and intricate mechanical detail

## Design Aesthetic

Richly detailed retro-futuristic world where Victorian elegance meets industrial machinery. Brass and copper metalwork, exposed gears, and steam-powered mechanisms define every visual element. Warm antique tones and intricate mechanical details create an alternate-history atmosphere of invention and adventure.

## Background

- Color: Dark Mahogany (#2C1810) or Aged Brass (#8B7355)
- Texture: Riveted metal plating or weathered leather with brass studs

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Victorian industrial display like Copperplate Gothic or Cinzel Decorative. Mechanical precision meets ornamental flair. Engraved letterpress quality.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Typewriter-style serif like Courier Prime or American Typewriter. Mechanical origin story in every character. Industrial-era authenticity.

## Visual Elements

- Intricate gear and cog illustrations
- Brass riveted border frames
- Exposed mechanical blueprints and schematics
- Pressure gauge and dial infographic elements
- Victorian ornamental flourishes
- Steam cloud and pipe connector graphics
- Clockwork mechanism backgrounds
- Airship and invention silhouettes

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use warm metallic tones (brass, copper, bronze)
- Include intricate mechanical detail in decorative elements
- Combine Victorian elegance with industrial machinery
- Create depth through layered mechanical textures
- Maintain an antique, hand-crafted quality

### Don't

- Use cold modern colors (neon, digital blue)
- Apply clean minimalist flat design
- Create smooth digital-looking surfaces
- Use contemporary sans-serif typefaces
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Maker movement presentations, invention showcases, alternative history talks, game design pitches, creative technology demos, engineering heritage stories, themed event proposals
