# Pop Art

Bold, vivid pop art style with flat colors, strong outlines, and graphic punch

## Design Aesthetic

Inspired by Warhol, Lichtenstein, and Haring, this style celebrates bold graphic simplicity. Flat, saturated colors are bounded by thick black outlines. Compositions are punchy, iconic, and instantly readable. The aesthetic is unapologetically loud, commercial, and culturally aware.

## Background

- Color: Hot White (#FFFEF5) or solid bright color block
- Texture: Flat with optional Ben-Day dot overlay

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Impact or similar ultra-bold condensed sans-serif. All caps, oversized, impossible to ignore. Comic-style lettering with thick outlines.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Roboto or similar clean sans-serif. Medium weight for readable contrast against loud visuals. Simple and direct.

## Visual Elements

- Thick black outlines around all shapes
- Flat color fills with no gradients
- Ben-Day dot patterns for texture
- Speech bubble and comic panel layouts
- Halftone screen effects
- Bold graphic icons and symbols
- Repetition and grid arrangements of motifs
- High-contrast figure-ground relationships

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use maximum saturation for all colors
- Outline everything with consistent thick black lines
- Keep compositions flat and two-dimensional
- Make text bold, loud, and integral to the design
- Embrace repetition and pattern

### Don't

- Use subtle or muted color palettes
- Add realistic shadows or 3D effects
- Include photographic imagery
- Use thin delicate line work
- Create complex layered compositions

## Best For

Marketing presentations, brand launches, youth-oriented talks, media and culture lectures, creative agency pitches, entertainment industry decks
