# Art Deco

Sleek geometric elegance with bold symmetry, metallic accents, and 1920s luxury

## Design Aesthetic

Opulent and commanding compositions defined by bold geometric forms, symmetrical patterns, and lavish metallic finishes. The visual language of the Roaring Twenties meets modern graphic precision: sunburst rays, stepped forms, and chevron patterns create a sense of aspirational glamour and machine-age confidence.

## Background

- Color: Deep Black (#0C0C0C)
- Texture: Smooth lacquered surface with subtle metallic sheen gradient

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Poiret One or similar Art Deco geometric display font. Thin, precise letterforms with distinctive geometric character. Glamorous and era-defining.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Raleway or similar geometric sans-serif for body text. Clean, modern lines that echo Deco geometry while maintaining readability.

## Visual Elements

- Sunburst and radiating line patterns
- Stepped geometric forms and ziggurats
- Chevron and fan motifs in repeating patterns
- Bold symmetrical compositions with central axis
- Metallic gold and silver border treatments
- Tall, narrow proportions in decorative elements
- Egyptian and Aztec-inspired geometric motifs
- Streamlined curves meeting sharp angles

## Style Rules

### Do

- Maintain strict bilateral symmetry in layouts
- Use bold geometric patterns as decorative framework
- Apply metallic gold and silver accents liberally
- Create a sense of luxury and aspiration
- Use sleek, streamlined forms throughout

### Don't

- Include organic, hand-drawn, or rustic elements
- Use blurry, noisy, or low-contrast treatments
- Create asymmetric, casual compositions
- Apply muted, understated color palettes
- Include deformed or imprecise geometric forms

## Best For

Luxury brand presentations, Gatsby-era event planning, architecture and design talks, cocktail and hospitality branding, fashion history lectures, premium product launches
