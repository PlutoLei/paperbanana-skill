# Flat 2D Art

Clean two-dimensional illustrations with simple flat colors, no shading, and crisp modern aesthetics

## Design Aesthetic

Contemporary digital illustration style that embraces the flatness of the screen. No shadows, no gradients, no 3D effects -- just clean shapes and solid colors. Inspired by modern app design and editorial illustration, the style achieves sophistication through careful color selection and smart compositional choices rather than rendering complexity.

## Background

- Color: Soft White (#FAFAFA) or Light Warm Gray (#F2F0ED)
- Texture: Perfectly flat and smooth, no texture whatsoever

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Clean geometric sans-serif with even stroke weight. Modern and confident. Rounded terminals for friendliness or sharp terminals for professionalism depending on content.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Regular-weight sans-serif with open letterforms. Excellent screen readability. Neutral personality that adapts to content without competing with illustrations.

## Visual Elements

- Simple geometric shapes as building blocks for all illustrations
- Flat color fills with zero gradients or shadows
- Clean vector icons with consistent stroke or fill style
- Solid color backgrounds for content sections
- Simplified human figures with minimal detail
- Flat landscape or cityscape scene compositions
- Bold color blocking to create visual hierarchy
- Crisp edges on every element

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use only solid flat colors throughout
- Keep shapes simple and easily recognizable
- Maintain consistent geometric construction for all elements
- Use color contrast to create depth perception
- Ensure high readability through clean layouts

### Don't

- Apply shadows, gradients, or 3D effects
- Use textures or photographic elements
- Create complex detailed illustrations
- Mix flat and realistic rendering styles
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

App and product presentations, startup pitch decks, onboarding flows, marketing presentations, social media content previews, educational explainers, tech company all-hands
