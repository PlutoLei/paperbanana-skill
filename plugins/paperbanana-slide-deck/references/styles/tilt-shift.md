# Tilt-Shift

Miniature effect photography with selective focus, vibrant color, and toy-like perspective

## Design Aesthetic

Playful and captivating compositions that make real-world scenes appear as miniature models through selective focus manipulation. A narrow band of sharp focus surrounded by creamy bokeh blur creates the illusion of viewing a tiny diorama. Colors are pushed vivid and saturated, enhancing the toy-like quality.

## Background

- Color: Soft Sky Blue (#E3F2FD)
- Texture: Smooth with gentle out-of-focus bokeh blur suggesting shallow depth of field

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Baloo 2 or similar friendly rounded sans-serif. Playful, approachable letterforms that match the toy-like aesthetic. Cheerful and inviting.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Nunito Sans or similar soft sans-serif for body text. Clean and readable with a gentle, approachable quality.

## Visual Elements

- Narrow band of sharp focus with surrounding blur
- Creamy bokeh circles in out-of-focus areas
- Elevated bird's-eye view perspective
- Oversaturated, vivid color treatment
- Miniature model-like scene compositions
- Tiny vehicles, buildings, and figures
- Selective focus gradient from sharp to soft
- Vibrant, toy-like color grading

## Style Rules

### Do

- Apply selective focus with sharp center and blurred edges
- Use elevated or aerial perspective viewpoints
- Push color saturation for miniature effect
- Create scenes that read as tiny model worlds
- Maintain vibrant, cheerful color throughout

### Don't

- Use flat, evenly focused compositions
- Create noisy, deformed, or distorted imagery
- Apply low-contrast or desaturated treatments
- Use flat, featureless backgrounds
- Make unrealistic oversaturated color that clips

## Best For

Urban planning presentations, architectural model showcases, travel and tourism content, photography technique talks, playful brand storytelling, real estate overview presentations
