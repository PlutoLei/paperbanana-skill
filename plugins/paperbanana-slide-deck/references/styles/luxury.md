# Luxury

Elegant, sophisticated high-end style with luxurious refinement

## Design Aesthetic

Opulent and refined visual language conveying premium quality and exclusivity. Rich dark backgrounds paired with metallic accents create depth and sophistication. Every element communicates craftsmanship and attention to detail, evoking the feel of a five-star experience.

## Background

- Color: Deep Charcoal (#1C1C1E) or Rich Navy (#0D1B2A)
- Texture: Subtle brushed metal or soft leather grain texture

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Refined modern serif like Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond. Elegant letterforms with sophisticated contrast. Generous tracking for a premium feel.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Thin sans-serif like Montserrat Light or Lato Thin. Clean and legible with understated elegance.

## Visual Elements

- Gold or metallic foil accent lines and borders
- High-resolution product photography with dramatic lighting
- Generous negative space conveying exclusivity
- Subtle gradient overlays on dark backgrounds
- Elegant thin-line geometric frames
- Monogram or crest-style decorative motifs
- Soft vignetting and depth-of-field effects
- Minimal, intentional use of color accents

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use dark backgrounds to convey depth and richness
- Apply metallic accents sparingly for maximum impact
- Maintain generous whitespace and breathing room
- Use high-quality imagery exclusively
- Keep layouts clean and uncluttered

### Don't

- Use bright or neon colors
- Crowd elements together
- Use casual or playful typefaces
- Include low-resolution or stock-looking imagery
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Luxury brand presentations, high-end product launches, premium service proposals, executive briefings, investment decks, hospitality and real estate showcases
