# Ornate and Intricate

Richly decorative style with elaborate detail work, filigree, and ornamental flourishes

## Design Aesthetic

Maximalist and meticulously detailed, drawing from baroque, rococo, and illuminated manuscript traditions. Every surface can carry decoration -- from delicate filigree borders to intricate pattern fills. The overall impression is one of craftsmanship, luxury, and visual richness.

## Background

- Color: Ivory Parchment (#FFFBF0)
- Texture: Subtle aged vellum with faint embossed pattern

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Cormorant Garamond or similar ornate serif. Display weight with elegant swashes and ligatures. Conveys luxury and historical gravitas.

### Secondary Font (Body)

EB Garamond or similar classical serif. Readable and refined, with traditional proportions that complement the decorative surroundings.

## Visual Elements

- Intricate filigree borders and corner ornaments
- Scrollwork and vine-like decorative motifs
- Detailed pattern fills within shapes
- Symmetrical mandala-like compositions
- Layered frames with diminishing detail scales
- Floral and botanical ornamental elements
- Gilded edge effects and embossed textures
- Illuminated capital letters for section headers

## Style Rules

### Do

- Layer detail at multiple scales (macro to micro)
- Maintain symmetry and balance in ornamental elements
- Use gold and metallic tones for richness
- Frame content with decorative borders
- Keep the craftsmanship feel consistent throughout

### Don't

- Use flat, minimalist layouts
- Include modern sans-serif typography
- Apply neon or electric color palettes
- Leave large areas of empty white space
- Mix in contemporary UI patterns

## Best For

Luxury brand presentations, cultural heritage talks, wedding and event design, historical lectures, manuscript and book arts, jewelry and craft showcases
