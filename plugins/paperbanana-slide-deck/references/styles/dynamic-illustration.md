# Dynamic Illustration

Energetic, expressive illustration with careless brushstrokes and emotional intensity

## Design Aesthetic

Wild, kinetic illustration style driven by raw emotion and spontaneous energy. Careless yet intentional brushstrokes create a sense of urgency and fun. Colors collide in unexpected ways. Nothing is static; every element vibrates with movement and personality. The aesthetic celebrates artistic abandon and unrestrained creative expression.

## Background

- Color: Warm White (#FDF8F0) or Vivid Accent Ground (varies per slide)
- Texture: Rough paper with visible paint splatters and drip marks

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Hand-lettered or rough brush script with energetic, uneven baseline. Bold, expressive, slightly chaotic. Feels hand-painted with urgency and personality.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Casual rounded sans-serif or informal handwriting face. Readable but never stiff. Maintains the loose, playful energy of the overall style.

## Visual Elements

- Energetic brushstrokes with visible speed and direction
- Paint splatters and drip marks as expressive accents
- Bold outlines with intentionally uneven line weight
- Overlapping color fields with transparency
- Exaggerated character poses and expressions
- Arrows and motion lines conveying wild movement
- Hand-scrawled annotations and exclamation marks
- Collage-like layering of illustrated elements

## Style Rules

### Do

- Embrace imperfection and spontaneous mark-making
- Use bold, saturated, clashing colors fearlessly
- Create movement and energy in every composition
- Allow brushstrokes to be visible and expressive
- Maintain a sense of fun, emotion, and creative chaos

### Don't

- Create static, stiff, or overly composed layouts
- Use photographic or hyper-realistic rendering
- Apply slow, boring, or predictable compositions
- Polish away all roughness and spontaneity
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Creative workshops, brainstorming sessions, youth and education content, startup culture presentations, entertainment pitches, art and design talks, high-energy event decks
