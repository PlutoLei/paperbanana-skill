# Expressionism

Raw emotional intensity with exaggerated forms, bold contrasts, and dynamic movement

## Design Aesthetic

Visceral and emotionally charged visual language that distorts reality to convey inner experience. Inspired by Munch, Kirchner, and Kandinsky, compositions feature exaggerated forms, aggressive brushwork, and jarring color contrasts. Movement and tension pervade every element, creating an urgent, psychologically intense atmosphere.

## Background

- Color: Dark Prussian Blue (#0B1D3A) or Storm Gray (#2F2F2F)
- Texture: Heavy impasto-style brushwork with visible thick paint texture

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Rough, hand-painted or woodcut-style lettering with irregular edges. High emotional impact through distorted or stretched forms. Raw and unpolished, matching the expressionist energy.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Bold sans-serif with slightly condensed proportions. Strong enough to hold its ground against intense visuals. Dark and commanding with clear readability.

## Visual Elements

- Thick visible brushstrokes with directional energy
- Exaggerated and distorted figure forms
- Jagged, angular lines conveying tension
- Swirling patterns suggesting emotional turbulence
- High-contrast color clashes placed deliberately
- Woodcut-style black outlines with rough edges
- Dynamic diagonal compositions implying movement
- Emotionally charged facial expressions and gestures

## Style Rules

### Do

- Exaggerate forms to amplify emotional content
- Use jarring color combinations for psychological impact
- Apply visible, aggressive brushwork textures
- Create dynamic tension through asymmetry and diagonals
- Let raw emotion drive compositional choices

### Don't

- Aim for realistic proportions or perspectives
- Use gentle pastels or harmonious palettes
- Create static, symmetrical compositions
- Apply clean digital precision
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Psychology and mental health topics, social justice presentations, dramatic storytelling, film critique sessions, protest and activism content, emotional brand narratives, contemporary art discussions
