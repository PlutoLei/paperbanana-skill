# Digital Art

Concept art and digital painting with illustrative, painterly matte painting quality

## Design Aesthetic

Professional digital concept art combining illustrative painting techniques with the polish of matte painting. Rich, detailed environments and characters rendered with painterly brushwork and careful color design. The style bridges traditional illustration and modern digital artistry, producing imagery that feels both handcrafted and production-ready for film, gaming, or publishing.

## Background

- Color: Atmospheric Gray (#2C2C34) or Concept Canvas (#1E222A)
- Texture: Subtle digital canvas with soft painterly strokes visible at edges

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Bold display serif or stylized sans-serif with character. Strong presence that complements the illustrative artwork without dominating. Often embossed or textured to match the painterly feel.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean sans-serif at medium weight. Readable and functional, grounding the illustrative content with professional typography.

## Visual Elements

- Painterly digital brushwork with visible artistic process
- Rich, layered environments with atmospheric perspective
- Character and creature concept illustrations
- Dramatic lighting with clear narrative mood
- Matte painting backgrounds with epic scale
- Detailed prop and object studies as visual anchors
- Color keys and mood thumbnails for variety
- Soft edge transitions blending into painterly backgrounds

## Style Rules

### Do

- Maintain painterly, illustrative brushwork quality
- Create rich, detailed environments with atmospheric depth
- Use dramatic lighting to establish mood and focal points
- Keep compositions highly detailed and visually dense
- Blend traditional illustration feel with digital polish

### Don't

- Use photographic, photorealistic, or camera-captured imagery
- Create flat, minimal, or sterile compositions
- Apply ugly, crude, or amateur-quality rendering
- Lose the painterly quality in favor of hard edges
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Game development presentations, film and VFX concept reviews, fantasy and sci-fi worldbuilding, illustration portfolio showcases, creative industry pitches, entertainment design talks, art school lectures
