# Vintage Airline Poster

Classic mid-century aviation travel poster with pastel skies and elegant aircraft silhouettes

## Design Aesthetic

Evokes the golden age of air travel with romantic, sun-drenched destination scenes and sleek aircraft illustrations. Distressed textures and faded color palettes create nostalgic allure. Typography channels 1950s airline branding with confident, stylized letterforms.

## Background

- Color: Aged Cream (#F5ECD7) or Faded Sky Blue (#C8DDE6)
- Texture: Lightly distressed paper with subtle fold creases and offset-print grain

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Bold condensed sans-serif or Art Deco display face. Confident, slightly rounded letterforms reminiscent of Pan Am and TWA branding. Often set in all-caps with generous tracking.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean geometric sans-serif. Functional and readable, echoing mid-century travel brochure copy.

## Visual Elements

- Stylized aircraft illustrations in profile or three-quarter view
- Scenic destination backdrops with simplified landmark silhouettes
- Radiating sunburst patterns behind key focal points
- Distressed border frames with rounded corners
- Ribbon banners carrying taglines or route names
- Cloud formations rendered in soft, flat pastel shapes
- Diagonal composition lines suggesting motion and travel
- Small compass or globe motifs as decorative accents

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use warm, slightly desaturated pastel tones
- Apply subtle distressed or aged texture overlays
- Keep aircraft and landmarks as stylized illustrations, not photos
- Emphasize bold, confident typography with retro flair
- Maintain a sense of aspirational romance and adventure

### Don't

- Use hyper-realistic rendering or photographic elements
- Apply bold neon or saturated modern colors
- Use sleek, minimalist contemporary typefaces
- Clutter the composition with too many small details
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Travel and tourism presentations, aviation history content, retro brand pitches, destination marketing, hospitality industry decks, nostalgic storytelling
