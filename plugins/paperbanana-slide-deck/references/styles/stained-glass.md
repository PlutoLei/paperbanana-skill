# Stained Glass

Vibrant translucent art style with intricate lead-line patterns and luminous color

## Design Aesthetic

Radiant compositions inspired by cathedral stained glass windows. Bold black outlines define segments of translucent, jewel-toned color that appear to glow with backlit luminosity. Intricate geometric and organic patterns create visual richness while maintaining clear structure.

## Background

- Color: Warm Black (#1C1C1E)
- Texture: Subtle dark stone or cathedral wall texture suggesting the frame around illuminated panels

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Trajan Pro or similar Roman-inspired serif. Uppercase letterforms with classical proportions. Monumental and sacred in presence.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Alegreya or similar humanist serif for body text. Warm, readable, with calligraphic roots. Evokes manuscript tradition.

## Visual Elements

- Bold black lead-line outlines defining all shapes
- Jewel-toned translucent color fills
- Backlit glow effect suggesting light through glass
- Geometric rosette and medallion patterns
- Gothic arch and trefoil framing elements
- Gradient fills within segments simulating glass thickness
- Radial symmetry in decorative compositions
- Mosaic-style tessellation for backgrounds

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use bold black outlines to define every color segment
- Create luminous, saturated color fills
- Simulate backlit translucency with subtle gradients
- Use geometric symmetry for decorative patterns
- Maintain intricate detail within structured composition

### Don't

- Use photorealistic or photographic elements
- Apply blurry, noisy, or low-contrast treatments
- Create flat designs without luminous depth
- Use muted, desaturated palettes
- Leave large areas of plain, undetailed space

## Best For

Art and art history presentations, religious and spiritual content, architectural showcases, cultural heritage talks, decorative arts exhibitions, premium brand storytelling
