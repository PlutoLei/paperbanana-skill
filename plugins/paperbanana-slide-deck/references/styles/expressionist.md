# Expressionist

Raw, emotionally charged style with dynamic distortion and vibrant unusual colors

## Design Aesthetic

Visceral and emotionally intense visual language where feeling takes precedence over form. Bold distortions, aggressive brush work, and unexpected color combinations create psychological tension and energy. Every element is pushed beyond realism to amplify emotional resonance and inner experience.

## Background

- Color: Deep Midnight (#1A1A2E) or Stormy Gray (#3A3A3A)
- Texture: Heavy impasto paint texture with visible thick brush strokes

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Rough hand-drawn or distressed display type like Permanent Marker or Amatic SC Bold. Raw, unrefined letterforms conveying emotional intensity. Irregular baselines.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Sturdy humanist sans-serif like Fira Sans or Merriweather Sans. Grounded and readable, anchoring the visual intensity.

## Visual Elements

- Thick aggressive brush strokes as graphic elements
- Distorted human figures and faces
- Jagged angular lines conveying tension
- Color fields that clash intentionally
- Dripping and splattered paint effects
- Stark light/shadow contrasts (chiaroscuro)
- Woodcut-style illustrations with rough marks
- Swirling, turbulent background textures

## Style Rules

### Do

- Distort forms to amplify emotional content
- Use bold, unexpected color combinations
- Create visible brush stroke energy in all elements
- Push contrast to dramatic extremes
- Let raw emotion drive composition choices

### Don't

- Create calm, balanced symmetrical layouts
- Use clean digital precision or sharp edges
- Apply pastel or muted harmonious palettes
- Use photographic imagery unaltered
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Emotional storytelling presentations, mental health awareness talks, music and performance art decks, film and theater pitches, provocative brand campaigns, social commentary presentations
