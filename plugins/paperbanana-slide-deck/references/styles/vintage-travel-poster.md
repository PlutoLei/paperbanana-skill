# Vintage Travel Poster

Retro destination poster style with muted colors, scenic illustrations, and nostalgic charm

## Design Aesthetic

Romanticized travel illustration in the tradition of WPA and European railway posters. Iconic landmarks rendered in simplified, painterly flat color. Distressed textures and muted palettes conjure a sense of mid-century wanderlust and hand-printed authenticity.

## Background

- Color: Warm Parchment (#F0E6D2) or Dusty Ivory (#EDE5D4)
- Texture: Vintage lithograph grain with subtle cracking and age spots

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Tall condensed serif or slab-serif with vintage character. Strong vertical emphasis, often all-caps. Reminiscent of 1930s-1950s European travel posters.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Rounded geometric sans-serif. Friendly, readable, slightly retro. Complements headline drama without competing.

## Visual Elements

- Simplified landmark silhouettes in flat color planes
- Layered landscape depth with foreground, middle, and background tiers
- Decorative typographic banners and arched text paths
- Sun ray or gradient sky behind key scenes
- Stylized trees, mountains, and water in broad strokes
- Thin ruled border frames with corner ornaments
- Small emblem or crest motifs for authenticity
- Subtle halftone dot pattern in shadow areas

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use muted, slightly desaturated earth tones
- Render scenes as flat-color illustrations with minimal shading
- Apply distressed paper or print textures for authenticity
- Keep typography bold, prominent, and period-appropriate
- Maintain a sense of quiet grandeur and scenic beauty

### Don't

- Use vibrant neon or hyper-modern color schemes
- Include photographic or hyper-realistic imagery
- Use sleek, thin, or minimalist modern typefaces
- Overcrowd the frame with competing visual elements
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Travel and destination content, cultural heritage presentations, geography education, museum exhibits, hospitality branding, tourism marketing, historical storytelling
