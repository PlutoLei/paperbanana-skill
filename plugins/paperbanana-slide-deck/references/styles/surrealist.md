# Surrealist

Dreamlike, mysterious style with provocative symbolic imagery and intricate detail

## Design Aesthetic

Enigmatic and psychologically charged visual language inspired by Dali, Magritte, and the Surrealist movement. Impossible juxtapositions, melting forms, and symbolic imagery create dreamlike compositions that defy logic. Every slide invites viewers into a subconscious landscape where the familiar becomes strange and the strange becomes meaningful.

## Background

- Color: Twilight Blue (#1A1A3E) or Desert Tan (#D4C5A9)
- Texture: Soft gradient sky or infinite horizon with subtle atmospheric haze

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Elegant serif with dreamlike quality like Bodoni or EB Garamond. Classical refinement that contrasts with surreal imagery. Deliberate and measured.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean humanist sans-serif like Source Sans Pro or Lato. Grounding rationality against irrational visuals.

## Visual Elements

- Impossible object combinations and scale distortions
- Melting, dripping, or morphing forms
- Floating objects defying gravity
- Vast empty desert or sky landscapes
- Eyes, clocks, and symbolic recurring motifs
- Shadow figures and silhouettes
- Mirror and reflection paradoxes
- Hyper-detailed objects in impossible contexts

## Style Rules

### Do

- Create unexpected juxtapositions of familiar objects
- Use vast empty spaces to amplify strangeness
- Maintain photorealistic detail in surreal compositions
- Let symbolic imagery carry layered meaning
- Embrace paradox and visual impossibility

### Don't

- Create logical, expected compositions
- Use busy cluttered layouts
- Apply cartoon or comic-style rendering
- Use bright cheerful color palettes
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Creative thinking workshops, psychology presentations, dream analysis talks, philosophy lectures, avant-garde brand campaigns, film and narrative pitches, conceptual art exhibitions
