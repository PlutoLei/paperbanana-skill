# Abstract

Non-representational art style with expressive colors, shapes, and imaginative compositions

## Design Aesthetic

Bold non-representational compositions that prioritize color relationships, geometric and organic forms, and emotional expression over literal depiction. Each slide feels like a canvas where shapes and colors communicate ideas through feeling rather than form. Imaginative and thought-provoking.

## Background

- Color: Off-White (#F8F6F0) or Soft Warm Gray (#EDEAE5)
- Texture: Canvas grain or subtle paper texture with visible weave

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Geometric display sans-serif like Futura Bold or Arquitecta. Clean forms that complement abstract shapes. Strong visual presence without competing with artwork.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Neutral humanist sans-serif like Gill Sans or Cabin. Balanced and readable, providing grounding contrast to abstract visuals.

## Visual Elements

- Bold geometric shapes (circles, triangles, rectangles) in layered compositions
- Organic free-form blobs and fluid shapes
- Overlapping translucent color fields
- Thick painted brush strokes as dividers
- Abstract texture fills and gradients
- Scattered dots and stipple patterns
- Contrasting shape scales for visual tension
- Color field backgrounds for sections

## Style Rules

### Do

- Let color and shape carry the emotional message
- Use bold contrasts between warm and cool tones
- Layer shapes with varying opacity for depth
- Embrace asymmetry and dynamic composition
- Allow visual elements to suggest rather than depict

### Don't

- Use photographic or realistic imagery
- Create rigid, grid-locked symmetric layouts
- Rely on literal icons or representational graphics
- Use muted monochromatic palettes
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Creative agency presentations, art gallery talks, design thinking workshops, brainstorming sessions, brand identity explorations, innovation pitches, conceptual strategy decks
