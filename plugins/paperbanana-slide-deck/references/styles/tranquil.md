# Tranquil

Calm, soothing visual atmosphere with peaceful colors and serene compositions

## Design Aesthetic

Every element is chosen to induce calm. Soft, desaturated colors flow gently across compositions. Shapes have rounded edges, spacing is generous, and nothing demands urgent attention. The visual rhythm is slow and meditative, like a still lake at dawn. Content breathes within open layouts that feel unhurried and restful.

## Background

- Color: Morning Mist (#F0F4F8)
- Texture: Barely perceptible soft gradient, like distant fog

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Quicksand Medium or similar soft rounded sans-serif. Gentle curves and open letterforms. Calming presence without sacrificing clarity.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Source Sans Pro Light or similar airy sans-serif. Generous x-height and open counters. Effortless to read at any size.

## Visual Elements

- Soft rounded shapes with no sharp corners
- Gentle gradients suggesting atmospheric depth
- Generous white space and breathing room
- Flowing organic curves and wave patterns
- Muted, desaturated color applications
- Soft-focus backgrounds suggesting distance
- Minimal ornamentation -- simplicity is calm
- Subtle shadow effects with low opacity

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use desaturated, low-chroma colors throughout
- Round all corners and soften all edges
- Provide abundant white space around every element
- Keep visual density low -- fewer elements, more rest
- Use gentle transitions and soft boundaries

### Don't

- Use bright saturated or neon colors
- Create sharp angular compositions
- Add busy patterns or dense textures
- Use bold or heavy font weights
- Include high-contrast or jarring elements

## Best For

Wellness and mindfulness talks, meditation guides, healthcare presentations, retreat planning, relaxation content, therapeutic program overviews
