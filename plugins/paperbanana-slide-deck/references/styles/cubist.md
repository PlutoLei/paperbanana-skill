# Cubist

Geometric, abstract style with fragmented perspectives and innovative composition

## Design Aesthetic

Fractured and multi-perspective visual language inspired by Picasso and Braque. Subjects are deconstructed into geometric planes viewed from multiple angles simultaneously. Angular forms overlap and intersect, creating visually complex compositions that challenge conventional perspective and reward careful observation.

## Background

- Color: Warm Parchment (#EDE5D8) or Muted Stone (#D9D2C5)
- Texture: Canvas weave with subtle paint crackle effect

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Angular geometric display type like Futura Bold or Bauhaus 93. Sharp edges and clean geometry echo cubist fragmentation. Strong visual weight.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean geometric sans-serif like Century Gothic or Jost. Structured and orderly, providing contrast to visual complexity.

## Visual Elements

- Fragmented geometric planes and angular shapes
- Overlapping faceted forms showing multiple viewpoints
- Grid lines and construction marks visible as design elements
- Collage-style text blocks at varied angles
- Muted earth-tone color fills in geometric sections
- Thick dark outlines defining fractured shapes
- Newspaper or text fragment textures in backgrounds
- Guitar, bottle, and still-life motif references

## Style Rules

### Do

- Fragment subjects into geometric planes
- Show multiple viewpoints simultaneously
- Use angular overlapping compositions
- Maintain a muted, earthy palette
- Let geometric structure drive layout decisions

### Don't

- Use photographic realism or smooth gradients
- Create conventional single-perspective views
- Apply bright saturated neon colors
- Use rounded or organic flowing shapes
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Design education talks, art history presentations, architecture proposals, innovation strategy decks, creative problem-solving workshops, deconstructive analysis presentations
