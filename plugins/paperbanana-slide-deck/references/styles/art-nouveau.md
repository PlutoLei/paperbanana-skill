# Art Nouveau

Elegant decorative style with flowing curvilinear forms and nature-inspired ornament

## Design Aesthetic

Organic and ornamental visual language inspired by the Art Nouveau movement of the late 19th century. Sinuous curves, botanical motifs, and flowing lines create an atmosphere of refined elegance. Every element feels hand-crafted with meticulous attention to decorative detail and natural beauty.

## Background

- Color: Antique Cream (#F5ECD7) or Sage Mist (#E8EDE4)
- Texture: Aged paper with subtle floral watermark patterns

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Art Nouveau display face like Arnold Bocklin, Eckmann, or decorative serif with organic curves. Flowing letterforms with whiplash curves and naturalistic details.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Elegant traditional serif like Garamond or Baskerville. Refined and readable with classical proportions that complement ornamental headlines.

## Visual Elements

- Sinuous flowing border frames with botanical motifs
- Stylized flower, leaf, and vine illustrations
- Whiplash curves as section dividers
- Stained glass-inspired color blocks
- Peacock feather and dragonfly decorative accents
- Organic flowing line work connecting elements
- Decorative initial caps and drop caps
- Symmetrical ornamental header and footer bands

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use flowing organic curves in all decorative elements
- Incorporate nature-inspired motifs (flowers, vines, insects)
- Maintain elegant symmetry in ornamental frames
- Let decorative borders frame content gracefully
- Use muted, earthy tones with jewel-tone accents

### Don't

- Use sharp geometric or angular shapes
- Apply modern minimalist flat design
- Use bright neon or artificial colors
- Create cluttered layouts that bury content
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Heritage brand presentations, museum and gallery talks, botanical or nature content, decorative arts showcases, luxury hospitality proposals, cultural event promotions, artisan product launches
