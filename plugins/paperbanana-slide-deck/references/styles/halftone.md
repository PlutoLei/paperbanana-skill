# Halftone

Retro halftone print aesthetic with dot matrix patterns, newspaper texture, and pop culture energy

## Design Aesthetic

Inspired by mid-century newspaper printing and Roy Lichtenstein's pop art appropriation of the technique. Images are rendered through graduated dot patterns that create tonal variation -- larger dots for shadows, smaller for highlights. The aesthetic is simultaneously nostalgic and graphically bold, bridging fine art and mass media with distinctive visual texture.

## Background

- Color: Newsprint Off-White (#F5F0E8)
- Texture: Visible halftone dot screen at macro level

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Anton or similar bold condensed display sans-serif. Newspaper headline energy. All caps for maximum tabloid impact.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Courier Prime or similar monospace font. Typewriter aesthetic that reinforces the print media theme.

## Visual Elements

- Graduated dot patterns creating tonal images
- Visible circular dots at various sizes
- Moiré patterns from overlapping dot screens
- Color separation visible as distinct dot layers
- High-contrast black and white base imagery
- Ben-Day dot fills for color areas
- Newspaper column and grid layouts
- Ink bleed and press artifacts for authenticity

## Style Rules

### Do

- Render all imagery through halftone dot patterns
- Use high contrast as the foundation
- Show visible dot structure at presentation scale
- Limit colors to 2-3 per composition (like print separations)
- Embrace the graphic, mass-media quality

### Don't

- Use smooth continuous-tone gradients
- Apply photographic realism
- Include subtle or muted color transitions
- Make dots too small to be visible
- Mix in digital effects that break the print illusion
