# Neon Noir

Cyberpunk neon-lit streets with dark rain-soaked atmosphere, vibrant signs, and high-contrast glow

## Design Aesthetic

Electric and brooding compositions set in rain-drenched urban nightscapes illuminated by neon. Vibrant pinks, blues, and cyans cut through deep darkness, reflecting off wet surfaces and creating a world that is simultaneously beautiful and ominous. The visual language merges film noir shadow play with cyberpunk luminosity.

## Background

- Color: Wet Asphalt Black (#0A0E17)
- Texture: Rain-streaked dark surface with distant neon reflections and fog

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Chakra Petch or similar angular, futuristic sans-serif. Sharp geometric letterforms with a cyberpunk edge. Electric and dangerous.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Fira Code or similar monospace for body text. Terminal-style readability evoking hacker interfaces and digital readouts.

## Visual Elements

- Glowing neon sign text and symbols
- Rain droplets and wet surface reflections
- Fog and atmospheric haze catching colored light
- Dark alleyway and urban street compositions
- Neon light bloom and lens flare effects
- Puddle reflections doubling neon colors
- Wire and cable silhouettes against lit backgrounds
- High-contrast shadows with colored edge lighting

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use deep darkness as the dominant canvas
- Create vibrant neon glow effects with bloom
- Show wet surface reflections amplifying color
- Maintain high contrast between dark and luminous
- Build atmospheric depth with fog and rain

### Don't

- Use bright, sunny, or daytime settings
- Create low-contrast, washed-out compositions
- Apply black-and-white monochrome treatments
- Include sketch, watercolor, or painterly styles
- Use flat lighting without atmospheric effects

## Best For

Cyberpunk world-building, gaming presentations, nightlife brand content, technology noir narratives, urban photography showcases, futuristic concept art displays
