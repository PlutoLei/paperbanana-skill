# Marker Drawing

Bold hand-drawn marker illustrations with thick lines, vivid color fills, and visible paper texture

## Design Aesthetic

Energetic hand-made aesthetic that celebrates the bold, confident strokes of marker pens on paper. Thick outlines in black marker frame areas of vivid color applied with visible directional strokes. The paper texture shows through, and slight imperfections in line work add authentic hand-crafted charm. Dynamic, accessible, and creatively energizing.

## Background

- Color: Warm Paper White (#FBF7F0) or Light Kraft (#F0E8D8)
- Texture: Visible marker paper or cardstock texture with subtle tooth

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Bold hand-lettered or marker-style font with thick strokes. Slight baseline irregularity and natural variation suggesting actual marker writing. Confident and expressive.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Rounded sans-serif with medium weight. Friendly and readable while maintaining hand-made cohesion. Consistent enough for longer text blocks.

## Visual Elements

- Thick black marker outlines on all illustrated elements
- Visible directional marker strokes in color fills
- Slight color bleed at edges where marker meets paper
- Hand-drawn arrows, stars, and emphasis marks
- Underlines and circles around key words
- Whiteboard-style diagrams and flowcharts
- Sketch-style icons with bold, simplified forms
- Paper texture visible through semi-transparent marker areas

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use bold consistent outline weight for all elements
- Show visible marker stroke direction in fills
- Keep illustrations simple and sketch-like
- Let paper texture show through color areas
- Maintain energetic, confident line quality

### Don't

- Use smooth digital gradients or airbrushed effects
- Apply photographic textures or images
- Create finely detailed or realistic illustrations
- Use thin or delicate line weights
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Brainstorming session recaps, creative workshop materials, ideation presentations, startup whiteboard pitches, youth education content, team retrospectives, rapid prototyping showcases
