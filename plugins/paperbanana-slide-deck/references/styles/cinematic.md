# Cinematic

Film still aesthetic with shallow depth of field, moody grading, and epic widescreen framing

## Design Aesthetic

Emulates the look of a high-budget cinematic film still. Shallow depth of field with gorgeous bokeh isolates subjects against atmospheric backgrounds. Moody color grading, film grain, and cinemascope framing create a sense of narrative weight and visual sophistication. Every slide feels like a carefully composed frame from an award-winning film.

## Background

- Color: Moody Dark (#141416) or Warm Shadow (#1E1A18)
- Texture: Fine film grain with subtle anamorphic lens artifacts

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Elegant thin serif or refined sans-serif with cinematic pedigree. Often set in all-caps with wide letter spacing. Understated yet commanding, like film title credits.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean light-weight sans-serif. Subtle and readable against atmospheric backgrounds. Often uses slight drop shadow for legibility.

## Visual Elements

- Shallow depth of field with beautiful bokeh backgrounds
- Cinemascope aspect ratio framing (2.39:1)
- Moody, atmospheric color grading with teal-orange split toning
- Fine film grain for organic texture
- Vignette and subtle lens flare effects
- Gorgeous backlit silhouettes and rim lighting
- Anamorphic lens characteristics: horizontal flare streaks
- Rich shadow detail with lifted black levels

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use shallow depth of field and bokeh consistently
- Apply moody, atmospheric color grading
- Frame compositions in widescreen cinematic ratio
- Include film grain for organic, tactile quality
- Create a sense of narrative tension and visual storytelling

### Don't

- Use flat, evenly lit, or clinical imagery
- Apply anime, cartoon, graphic, or painted styles
- Create text-heavy compositions that break the cinematic feel
- Use abstract, glitchy, or deformed visual elements
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Film and media presentations, narrative storytelling, brand story decks, photography showcases, mood and concept boards, pitch decks for creative projects, awards and recognition ceremonies
