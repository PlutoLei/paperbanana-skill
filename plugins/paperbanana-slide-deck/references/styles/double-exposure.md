# Double Exposure

Ethereal layered imagery where two scenes merge into a single ghostly composition

## Design Aesthetic

Photographic technique turned artistic style, where two distinct images are blended into one haunting composition. Silhouettes filled with landscapes, cityscapes, or abstract textures create dreamlike double meanings. The layered transparency effect evokes mystery, duality, and narrative depth. Moody, cinematic, and emotionally resonant.

## Background

- Color: Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A) or Misty White (#F0F0F0)
- Texture: Smooth photographic finish with subtle film grain

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Thin elegant sans-serif with generous spacing. Ethereal and understated to complement the layered imagery. Light weight that floats above the visual complexity beneath.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean sans-serif in light or regular weight. Restrained and minimal so as not to compete with the photographic layers. High legibility against both light and dark backgrounds.

## Visual Elements

- Silhouette profiles filled with secondary imagery
- Transparent layered landscapes within figures
- Film grain and analog photography texture
- Soft light leaks and lens flare accents
- Ghostly double images with offset alignment
- Fading edges dissolving into background
- Monochromatic base with selective warm or cool tints
- Cinematic wide-format compositions

## Style Rules

### Do

- Blend exactly two visual layers with meaningful contrast
- Use transparency and opacity as primary compositional tools
- Create narrative through the juxtaposition of layers
- Maintain a moody, atmospheric tone
- Allow images to fade and dissolve at edges

### Don't

- Use sharp borders or hard-edged frames
- Apply bright flat colors or solid fills
- Include text-heavy layouts that obscure imagery
- Mix more than three layers (loses clarity)
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Film and photography showcases, brand identity reveals, environmental storytelling, memoir and biography presentations, music album artwork, cinematic pitch decks, creative portfolio reviews
