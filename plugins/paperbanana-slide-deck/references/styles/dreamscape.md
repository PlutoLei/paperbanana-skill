# Dreamscape

Surreal, ethereal style with dreamy atmosphere, mysterious depth, and fantasy elements

## Design Aesthetic

Ethereal and otherworldly visual language that dissolves the boundary between waking and dreaming. Soft-focus environments, floating elements, and impossible landscapes create a gentle sense of wonder. Luminous atmospheric effects and muted fantasy colors wrap every composition in a misty, contemplative tranquility.

## Background

- Color: Misty Lavender (#E8E0F0) or Deep Dream Blue (#1A1A3E)
- Texture: Soft atmospheric fog gradients with subtle particle dust

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Ethereal light-weight serif like Cormorant Light or EB Garamond Italic. Delicate and floating, as if written in mist. Gentle curves and graceful proportions.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Soft sans-serif like Nunito Light or DM Sans. Gentle and approachable with rounded terminals. Whisper-quiet presence.

## Visual Elements

- Soft atmospheric fog and mist layers
- Floating islands, objects, and structures
- Luminous particle and firefly effects
- Impossible architecture and gravity-defying scenes
- Aurora and celestial light phenomena
- Reflection pools and mirror surfaces
- Gentle lens flares and bokeh light effects
- Silhouette figures in vast dreamlike spaces

## Style Rules

### Do

- Create soft, atmospheric depth with fog layers
- Use gentle luminous light as a primary element
- Allow elements to float and defy physics
- Maintain a contemplative, peaceful mood
- Blend edges softly between elements

### Don't

- Use harsh sharp edges or high contrast
- Create realistic, grounded environments
- Apply aggressive neon or saturated colors
- Include busy, cluttered compositions
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Mindfulness and wellness presentations, creative writing talks, fantasy world-building, meditation and spiritual content, children's story illustrations, conceptual art exhibitions, aspirational brand storytelling
