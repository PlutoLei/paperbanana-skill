# Neo-Futurism

High-tech futuristic aesthetic with flowing curves, spirals, and idealistic tomorrow-world visuals

## Design Aesthetic

Sleek, forward-looking compositions that merge organic curves with high-tech precision. Flowing lines and spirals convey motion and progress, evoking an idealistic vision of tomorrow. Clean yet dynamic, balancing technological sharpness with organic fluidity.

## Background

- Color: Deep Space (#0A0E1A)
- Texture: Subtle gradient glow with faint circuit-like grid lines

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Orbitron or similar geometric futuristic sans-serif. Bold weight with wide tracking. Conveys advanced technology and forward momentum.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Inter or similar clean sans-serif for readability. Light to regular weight. Modern and legible at all sizes.

## Visual Elements

- Flowing curved lines and spiral motifs
- Glowing neon edge highlights on shapes
- Gradient overlays suggesting light emission
- Smooth, aerodynamic forms and silhouettes
- Subtle particle or dot-field backgrounds
- Transparent glass-morphism panels
- Concentric arc patterns radiating outward
- Holographic or iridescent accent touches

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use sweeping curves and organic flowing lines
- Apply gradient glows for depth and energy
- Maintain high contrast between dark backgrounds and bright accents
- Keep compositions dynamic with diagonal and curved layouts
- Use transparency and layering for depth

### Don't

- Use sharp angular blocky layouts
- Include retro or vintage design elements
- Use muted or earthy color palettes
- Add heavy textures or grunge effects
- Clutter compositions with excessive detail

## Best For

Technology keynotes, innovation presentations, startup pitches, product launches, science fiction concepts, futuristic architecture
