# Long Exposure

Dreamy motion-blur photography style with light streaks, surreal ghosting, and fluid movement

## Design Aesthetic

Ethereal and dreamlike compositions that capture the passage of time in a single frame. Flowing light trails, silky water, and ghostly motion blur transform ordinary scenes into surreal visual poetry. Static elements remain sharp while moving elements become streaks of luminous energy.

## Background

- Color: Twilight Blue (#1A237E)
- Texture: Smooth gradient suggesting a long-exposure sky, from deep blue to soft horizon glow

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Prata or similar elegant high-contrast serif. Flowing, graceful letterforms that echo the fluid motion of long exposure. Dreamy and refined.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Karla or similar light sans-serif for body text. Soft and unobtrusive, maintaining the ethereal mood.

## Visual Elements

- Flowing light trail streaks across compositions
- Silky smooth water surfaces from extended exposure
- Ghosting effects on moving subjects
- Star trail circular patterns in sky areas
- Vehicle light trails creating luminous paths
- Soft motion blur on organic elements
- Sharp static elements contrasting with blurred motion
- Dreamy cloud streaks across skylines

## Style Rules

### Do

- Create fluid, flowing motion in dynamic elements
- Contrast sharp stillness with blurred movement
- Use light trails as compositional leading lines
- Maintain a dreamy, surreal atmospheric quality
- Apply smooth, creamy blur to moving elements

### Don't

- Make everything static and frozen
- Include noisy, shaky, or abrupt elements
- Create flat, low-contrast compositions
- Use deformed or distorted imagery
- Apply sharp, crisp detail to all elements uniformly

## Best For

Photography technique presentations, cityscape and travel showcases, time and motion studies, artistic portfolio reviews, astronomy and night sky talks, meditative visual experiences
