# Craft Clay

Play-Doh sculpture and claymation style with tactile, handmade charm

## Design Aesthetic

Everything looks like it was lovingly sculpted from colorful Play-Doh or modeling clay. Rounded, slightly imperfect forms with visible fingerprint textures and smooth clay surfaces. Centered, approachable compositions with a childlike sense of wonder. The aesthetic is warm, tactile, and irresistibly charming, channeling stop-motion claymation magic.

## Background

- Color: Soft Clay White (#F0ECE4) or Pastel Studio (#E8E4DC)
- Texture: Subtle clay surface with smooth, matte finish and very faint fingerprint impressions

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Rounded, chunky sans-serif with Play-Doh-like thickness. Slightly uneven, as if molded by hand. Bold, friendly, and approachable with soft corners.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean rounded sans-serif. Simple, readable, maintaining the friendly handmade quality without competing with the clay visuals.

## Visual Elements

- Rounded, sculpted 3D forms with clay material appearance
- Visible fingerprint textures and hand-molded edges
- Centered compositions on clean studio-like surfaces
- Soft ambient lighting with gentle shadows
- Simple facial expressions on character sculptures
- Miniature scene dioramas with clay props and backdrops
- Smooth, matte surfaces without gloss or reflection
- Slightly imperfect shapes conveying handmade warmth

## Style Rules

### Do

- Make everything look sculpted from soft modeling clay
- Keep forms rounded, simple, and slightly imperfect
- Use bright, primary Play-Doh colors
- Center compositions with clean, uncluttered backgrounds
- Maintain a tactile, handmade quality in all elements

### Don't

- Use highly detailed, ultra-textured, or photorealistic rendering
- Create sloppy, messy, or grainy imagery
- Apply sharp edges or angular geometry
- Include photographic elements or flat 2D graphics
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Children's education, creative workshops, product concepts, playful brand presentations, stop-motion and animation talks, craft and maker content, family-friendly event decks
