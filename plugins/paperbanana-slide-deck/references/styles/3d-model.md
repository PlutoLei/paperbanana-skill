# 3D Model

Professional 3D renders with Octane-quality lighting, volumetric effects, and dramatic detail

## Design Aesthetic

Polished, studio-quality 3D rendering with the precision and realism of professional CGI. Dramatic volumetric lighting sculpts every surface, revealing rich material detail and depth. Octane-level render quality with clean geometry, physically accurate materials, and cinematic camera work. Every element feels tangible, meticulously crafted, and production-ready.

## Background

- Color: Studio Dark (#1A1A1E) or Gradient Gray (#2A2A30 to #0E0E12)
- Texture: Smooth gradient with subtle volumetric fog or ambient haze

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Modern geometric sans-serif with clean, sharp edges. Medium to bold weight. Often rendered with subtle 3D extrusion, metallic finish, or ambient occlusion shadow. Technical precision in every letterform.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Light-weight geometric sans-serif. Crisp and technical, rendered flat for readability against 3D environments.

## Visual Elements

- Photorealistic 3D objects with physically-based materials
- Three-point lighting setups with dramatic key and rim lights
- Volumetric fog and atmospheric haze for depth
- Reflective surfaces and caustic light effects
- Clean, precise geometry with smooth surfaces
- Depth of field with selective focus
- Ambient occlusion in crevices and contact shadows
- Subtle environment reflections on glossy surfaces

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use dramatic, directional lighting with clear light sources
- Maintain photorealistic material quality on all surfaces
- Create volumetric depth with fog, haze, or atmosphere
- Keep geometry clean, detailed, and precisely modeled
- Apply physically accurate reflections and refractions

### Don't

- Use flat, evenly lit, or shadowless rendering
- Create ugly, deformed, or low-polygon geometry
- Apply noisy, blurry, or unfinished render quality
- Use painterly, illustrative, or cartoon styles
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Product design presentations, architectural visualization, industrial design showcases, game and VFX breakdowns, technical demonstrations, engineering proposals, 3D portfolio reviews
