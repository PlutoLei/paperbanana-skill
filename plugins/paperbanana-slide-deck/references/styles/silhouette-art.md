# Silhouette Art

High-contrast silhouette compositions with bold figure-ground relationships

## Design Aesthetic

Dramatic and reductive, this style distills subjects to their essential outlines filled with solid black or dark color against light backgrounds (or vice versa). The power comes from what is left out -- detail is implied through shape alone. Every silhouette must be instantly recognizable, creating striking visual narratives through shadow and form.

## Background

- Color: Warm Sunset Gradient (#FEF3C7 to #FBBF24) or Clean White (#FFFFFF)
- Texture: Smooth, flat -- no texture to compete with silhouette edges

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Montserrat Bold or similar strong geometric sans-serif. Clean and authoritative. The typography must hold its own against bold silhouette imagery.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Open Sans or similar neutral sans-serif. Light to regular weight. Unobtrusive and functional.

## Visual Elements

- Solid black silhouette figures and objects
- Clean, precise silhouette edges
- Layered silhouettes creating depth planes
- Dramatic scenic compositions (trees, cityscapes, figures)
- Negative space used as active design element
- Horizon lines separating color zones
- Gradient skies behind silhouette foregrounds
- Minimal interior detail -- shape tells the story

## Style Rules

### Do

- Ensure every silhouette is immediately recognizable
- Maintain extreme contrast between figure and ground
- Use layered depth through overlapping silhouettes
- Keep edges clean and well-defined
- Let negative space carry visual weight

### Don't

- Add internal detail or texture to silhouettes
- Use low-contrast or muddy color combinations
- Include gradients within silhouette shapes
- Overcrowd compositions with too many elements
- Break the silhouette with partial transparency

## Best For

Storytelling presentations, theatrical event programs, nature and wildlife talks, motivational speeches, book cover design, evening event invitations
