# Cel Shaded Art

Bold flat-color illustration with crisp outlines and cartoon-style toon shading

## Design Aesthetic

Comic book and animation-inspired visual style with clearly defined outlines and flat areas of color. Two-tone shading creates a 3D illusion without gradients, mimicking traditional cel animation. Bold, energetic, and highly graphic with a playful yet polished feel that makes complex topics approachable.

## Background

- Color: Clean White (#FFFFFF) or Soft Sky Blue (#E8F4FD)
- Texture: Perfectly flat and smooth, no grain or noise

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Bold rounded sans-serif or comic-style lettering. Strong, chunky strokes with friendly character. Often features slight outlines or color fills to match the cel-shaded aesthetic.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Medium-weight sans-serif with rounded terminals. Clean and modern with good readability. Maintains the approachable, animated feel.

## Visual Elements

- Thick black outlines on every object and character
- Flat color fills with single-step shadow tones
- Comic-style speech bubbles and callout boxes
- Bold action lines and motion indicators
- Simplified character illustrations with expressive faces
- Flat color backgrounds with simple environmental cues
- Graphic panel layouts reminiscent of comic strips
- Clean iconography with consistent outline weight

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use uniform outline thickness on all elements
- Apply exactly two tones per color (base + shadow)
- Keep shapes simple and clearly readable
- Maintain high contrast between fill and outline
- Use flat colors only, no gradients

### Don't

- Apply soft shading or realistic lighting
- Use photographic textures or references
- Create detailed backgrounds that compete with foreground
- Mix outline weights inconsistently
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Gaming presentations, animation studio pitches, youth education, explainer content, comic-con events, product tutorials, app onboarding walkthroughs, creative workshops
