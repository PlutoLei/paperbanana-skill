# Chromolithography

Vintage multi-color lithographic print style with rich saturation, ornate detail, and nostalgic charm

## Design Aesthetic

Evokes the golden age of commercial printing (1870s-1920s) when chromolithography brought vibrant color to posters, labels, and advertisements. Rich color saturation from layered ink passes, meticulous registration creating crisp multi-color imagery, and ornate decorative compositions define this look. The result feels like a lovingly preserved Victorian-era poster.

## Background

- Color: Aged Paper (#F5EDDA)
- Texture: Subtle lithographic stone grain with faint printing artifacts

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Playfair Display SC or similar ornate display serif. Small caps for headers. Victorian-era typographic character with elegant proportions.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Crimson Text or similar traditional serif. Warm, readable, and historically appropriate.

## Visual Elements

- Multi-layered color printing with slight registration offset
- Ornate decorative borders and cartouches
- Detailed botanical and figurative illustrations
- Cross-hatching and stipple shading techniques
- Ribbon banners and scrollwork lettering
- Medallion and seal motifs
- Period-appropriate decorative frames
- Subtle color trapping effects at layer boundaries

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use rich, saturated colors with slight vintage muting
- Include ornate borders and decorative frames
- Show subtle misregistration for authenticity
- Layer colors as if from multiple print passes
- Maintain the craft and detail of period illustration

### Don't

- Use modern gradients or digital effects
- Apply flat minimalist layouts
- Include photographic imagery
- Use neon or electric color palettes
- Make compositions sparse or empty

## Best For

Heritage brand presentations, museum exhibitions, antique and vintage showcases, historical lectures, craft beer and artisan branding, botanical illustrations
