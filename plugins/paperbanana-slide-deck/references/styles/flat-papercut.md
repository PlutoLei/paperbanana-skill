# Flat Papercut

Clean silhouette art with sharp paper edges, bold color blocks, and minimalist composition

## Design Aesthetic

Crisp and graphic compositions that emulate flat paper cutouts arranged on a surface. Bold silhouettes with razor-sharp edges and solid color blocks create striking visual clarity. The aesthetic combines folk art simplicity with modern graphic design sensibility, using negative space and layered planes of color.

## Background

- Color: Soft Cream (#FFF8E1)
- Texture: Flat matte paper surface, no grain or noise

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Montserrat Black or similar geometric bold sans-serif. Strong, blocky letterforms that mirror the clean-cut paper shapes. Confident and graphic.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Open Sans or similar neutral sans-serif for body text. Clean and functional, complementing the flat graphic style.

## Visual Elements

- Flat silhouette shapes with perfectly sharp edges
- Layered color planes suggesting depth without 3D
- Bold negative space as compositional element
- Clean geometric and organic cutout forms
- Minimal shadow layers at slight offset
- Color block backgrounds divided by cut lines
- Folk art-inspired motifs (trees, animals, figures)
- No gradients within shapes; pure flat fill

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use sharp, clean edges on all shapes
- Maintain flat, solid color fills without gradients
- Create visual interest through shape and color contrast
- Use negative space deliberately and boldly
- Keep compositions simple and immediately readable

### Don't

- Add 3D effects, bevels, or realistic shading
- Use photographic or highly detailed elements
- Include noisy, grainy, or blurry textures
- Create drawings or painterly illustrations
- Apply complex patterns within shapes

## Best For

Children's education materials, cultural storytelling, graphic design showcases, book cover concepts, folk art exhibitions, clean brand presentations
