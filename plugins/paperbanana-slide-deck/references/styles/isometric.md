# Isometric

Vibrant isometric illustrations with crisp detail and intricate three-dimensional scenes

## Design Aesthetic

Precision isometric projection creates a playful yet technically impressive three-dimensional world. Vibrant colors, crisp lines, and intricate detail fill every scene with discoverable elements. The fixed 30-degree viewing angle provides consistency while allowing complex environments to unfold. Clean, beautiful, and endlessly detailed.

## Background

- Color: Soft White (#F8F8FA) or Light Lavender Gray (#EEEDF5)
- Texture: Clean, flat surface with subtle grid lines at isometric angles

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Bold geometric sans-serif with clean, precise letterforms. Often rendered in isometric perspective to match the visual style. Strong, modern, and vibrant.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Medium-weight sans-serif, flat and readable. Provides clear contrast to the dimensional illustrations. Clean and functional.

## Visual Elements

- Precise isometric projection at consistent 30-degree angles
- Intricate miniature scenes with rich environmental detail
- Clean vector-like edges with no blur or noise
- Bright, vibrant color blocking for different zones
- Small animated characters and activity throughout scenes
- Layered building and structure cutaways
- Connecting pathways, roads, and flow lines
- Shadow planes beneath floating isometric elements

## Style Rules

### Do

- Maintain strict isometric projection angles throughout
- Use vibrant, saturated, harmonious color combinations
- Pack scenes with crisp, discoverable details
- Keep edges ultra-clean and vector-sharp
- Create beautiful, inviting three-dimensional worlds

### Don't

- Use deformed, mutated, or ugly geometry
- Apply blur, noise, or realistic photographic effects
- Create flat 2D illustrations breaking the isometric feel
- Use dull, desaturated, or muddy colors
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Technology overviews, product ecosystem diagrams, smart city and urban planning, process flow explanations, architectural concepts, infographic-style data presentations, game design showcases
