# Fashion Editorial

High fashion editorial style with trendy, stylish magazine aesthetics

## Design Aesthetic

Sleek and sophisticated fashion magazine layout with bold editorial compositions. High-contrast imagery paired with refined typographic treatments. Exudes trend-forward confidence with curated visual storytelling that feels like flipping through Vogue or Harper's Bazaar.

## Background

- Color: Pure White (#FFFFFF) or Soft Ivory (#F5F0EB)
- Texture: Clean matte finish with subtle linen or silk texture overlays

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

High-contrast modern serif like Didot or Bodoni. Ultra-thin strokes with dramatic thick-thin contrast. All-caps for maximum editorial impact.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Light-weight geometric sans-serif like Futura Light or Avenir. Generous letter-spacing. Elegant and airy.

## Visual Elements

- Full-bleed editorial photography with dramatic lighting
- Oversized typography overlapping imagery
- Asymmetric grid layouts with generous whitespace
- Thin hairline rules and dividers
- Monochrome photography with selective color pops
- Pull quotes in elegant serif fonts
- Minimal iconography, imagery-driven
- Bold crop and framing choices

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use high-contrast black and white as foundation
- Embrace asymmetry and negative space
- Let typography be a visual element itself
- Use dramatic scale contrast between elements
- Maintain a curated, less-is-more approach

### Don't

- Clutter layouts with too many elements
- Use clip art or cartoon-style illustrations
- Apply drop shadows or beveled effects
- Use more than two typefaces per slide
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Fashion brand presentations, luxury product launches, creative agency pitches, portfolio showcases, trend reports, brand identity reveals, editorial content summaries
