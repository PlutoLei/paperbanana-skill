# Impressionism

Luminous painterly scenes with visible brushstrokes, dappled light, and fleeting atmospheric beauty

## Design Aesthetic

Light-filled compositions inspired by Monet, Renoir, and Degas that capture fleeting moments through color and brushwork rather than precise detail. Small, visible brushstrokes blend optically to create vibrant, shimmering surfaces. The emphasis on natural light, outdoor scenes, and atmospheric effects gives every slide a sense of ephemeral beauty and sensory richness.

## Background

- Color: Soft Linen (#F5F0E8) or Pale Lavender (#EDE7F6)
- Texture: Visible brushstroke canvas texture with a painterly grain

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Refined serif with moderate contrast and warm proportions. Classical elegance that complements rather than competes with painterly visuals. Slightly rounded serifs for softness.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Light-weight serif or humanist sans-serif. Gentle and readable with warm character. Maintains the artistic, unhurried feel of the compositions.

## Visual Elements

- Visible small brushstrokes creating shimmering surfaces
- Dappled light effects and sun-through-leaves patterns
- Soft-focus backgrounds with atmospheric haze
- Water reflection motifs with broken color
- Floral and garden scene compositions
- Broken color technique (adjacent pure hues blending optically)
- Soft vignettes fading at composition edges
- En plein air landscape fragments as decorative panels

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use small visible brushstrokes throughout illustrations
- Emphasize natural light and atmospheric effects
- Build color through optical mixing of adjacent hues
- Capture the impression of a moment rather than sharp detail
- Maintain luminous, light-saturated color values

### Don't

- Use hard outlines or crisp digital edges
- Apply flat solid color fills
- Create dark or heavily shadowed compositions
- Include sharp geometric shapes or mechanical elements
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Art appreciation lectures, wine and gastronomy events, garden party invitations, romantic brand storytelling, tourism and travel presentations, creative writing workshops, museum exhibition guides
