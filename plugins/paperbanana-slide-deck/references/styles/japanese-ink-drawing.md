# Japanese Ink Drawing

Meditative sumi-e aesthetic with flowing ink washes, deliberate brushwork, and elegant emptiness

## Design Aesthetic

Contemplative visual language rooted in Japanese sumi-e ink wash painting. Monochromatic compositions celebrate the expressive power of black ink on white paper. Deliberate brushstrokes range from bold and decisive to delicate and vanishing. The profound use of empty space (ma) carries as much meaning as the ink itself, creating serene, meditative compositions.

## Background

- Color: Rice Paper White (#F7F3EB) or Warm Washi (#FAF6EE)
- Texture: Handmade Japanese washi paper texture with visible fibers and soft absorbency

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Brush-style calligraphic font evoking East Asian calligraphy. Bold, expressive strokes with natural variation in thickness. May incorporate actual calligraphic characters as design elements.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Thin serif or minimal sans-serif with quiet elegance. Very light weight that doesn't compete with ink illustrations. Generous spacing for a contemplative reading pace.

## Visual Elements

- Bold single-stroke brushwork for key elements
- Graduated ink washes from dense to translucent
- Abundant negative space (ma) as compositional foundation
- Bamboo, pine, plum blossom, and mountain motifs
- Red seal stamp (hanko) as signature accent
- Flowing water and mist atmospheric effects
- Delicate branch and leaf silhouettes
- Ink splatter and drip marks as expressive accents

## Style Rules

### Do

- Honor empty space as an active design element
- Use the full tonal range of a single ink (pure black to faintest gray)
- Make each brushstroke deliberate and expressive
- Limit color to monochrome with rare vermillion accents
- Evoke nature through minimal, suggestive marks

### Don't

- Fill the entire composition with elements
- Use multiple bright colors
- Apply digital precision or mechanical lines
- Include photographic elements
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos
