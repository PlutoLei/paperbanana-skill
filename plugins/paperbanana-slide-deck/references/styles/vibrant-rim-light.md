# Vibrant Rim Light

Dramatic edge-lit compositions with bright rim lighting and bold contrast

## Design Aesthetic

Subjects emerge from dark backgrounds, dramatically defined by intense rim lighting along their edges. The bright contour light creates a striking halo effect that separates foreground from background with cinematic intensity. High contrast is the defining feature -- deep shadows meet brilliant edge highlights, producing images with theatrical depth and visual punch.

## Background

- Color: Deep Black (#0A0A0A)
- Texture: Smooth gradient falloff from edge light sources, no surface texture

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

DM Sans Bold or similar modern geometric sans-serif. Clean and strong against dark backgrounds. Slight glow effect for integration with rim-light aesthetic.

### Secondary Font (Body)

IBM Plex Sans or similar technical sans-serif. Regular weight with high legibility on dark backgrounds.

## Visual Elements

- Intense rim lighting along subject edges
- Deep black negative space
- Bright contour halos separating layers
- Colored light spill creating atmospheric glow
- Dramatic high-contrast figure-ground separation
- Subtle bounce light suggesting environment
- Light flares at key edge intersections
- Gradient falloff from bright edges into darkness

## Style Rules

### Do

- Keep backgrounds predominantly dark
- Make rim light the primary visual storytelling device
- Use colored rim lights for emotional variety
- Maintain extreme contrast ratios
- Let darkness be an active compositional element

### Don't

- Fill backgrounds with light or detail
- Use flat even lighting
- Add busy textures or patterns
- Reduce contrast with mid-tone fills
- Place too many light sources competing for attention

## Best For

Product photography showcases, cinematic presentations, music and entertainment pitches, luxury brand reveals, dramatic keynotes, portfolio showcases
