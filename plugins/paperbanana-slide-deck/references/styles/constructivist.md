# Constructivist

Bold propaganda art style with geometric shapes, dynamic composition, and revolutionary energy

## Design Aesthetic

Powerful and politically charged visual language drawn from Russian Constructivism. Bold diagonal compositions with stark geometric forms create urgency and movement. Limited color palette of red, black, and white dominates with industrial typography delivering messages with maximum impact and revolutionary fervor.

## Background

- Color: Off-White (#F2EFDF) or Industrial Tan (#E8DCC8)
- Texture: Rough newsprint or aged propaganda poster grain

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Bold condensed industrial sans-serif like Bebas Neue, League Gothic, or Oswald Black. All-caps with tight tracking. Arranged at dramatic angles for dynamic impact.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Sturdy geometric sans-serif like DIN or Roboto Condensed. Functional and mechanical. Industrial efficiency in every letterform.

## Visual Elements

- Bold diagonal lines and angular compositions
- Photomontage-style collage elements
- Geometric shapes (wedges, circles, bars) as layout tools
- Large-scale rotated typography as graphic elements
- Industrial icons (gears, hammers, lightning bolts)
- Strong figure silhouettes
- Layered red and black graphic blocks
- Radiating lines suggesting energy and movement

## Style Rules

### Do

- Tilt elements at dramatic angles (15-45 degrees)
- Use red and black as primary statement colors
- Create dynamic tension through diagonal composition
- Make typography a bold graphic element
- Embrace stark contrast and visual power

### Don't

- Use soft pastels or muted colors
- Create symmetrical, static compositions
- Apply photorealistic or decorative elements
- Use script or decorative typefaces
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Social movement presentations, political campaign materials, startup manifestos, disruptive brand launches, activist organizations, bold marketing campaigns, design history talks
