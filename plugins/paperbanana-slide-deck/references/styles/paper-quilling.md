# Paper Quilling

Delicate 3D paper art with intricate coiled strips, dimensional ornaments, and elegant craftsmanship

## Design Aesthetic

Exquisite three-dimensional compositions built from tightly coiled, curled, and shaped paper strips. Every element has visible dimensionality with delicate shadows cast by raised paper forms. The aesthetic balances ornamental richness with graceful restraint, creating patterns that are both intricate and harmonious.

## Background

- Color: Ivory (#FFFFF0)
- Texture: Smooth card stock with very subtle linen emboss

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Libre Baskerville or similar refined serif. Elegant curves echo the coiled forms of quilling. Sophisticated and graceful presence.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Raleway or similar thin-weight sans-serif for body text. Light and airy to complement the delicate paper forms.

## Visual Elements

- Tightly coiled paper spirals in various sizes
- Teardrop, marquise, and scroll shaped paper forms
- Visible dimensional shadows beneath raised elements
- Floral and botanical compositions from paper strips
- Graduated color coils showing paper edge detail
- Looping and curling decorative borders
- Layered depth with forms at multiple heights
- Delicate filigree patterns from thin paper strips

## Style Rules

### Do

- Emphasize three-dimensionality with realistic shadows
- Use delicate, intricate forms with fine detail
- Create ornamental patterns with visible paper texture
- Maintain a light, airy overall composition
- Show the curling and coiling of individual strips

### Don't

- Create flat, 2D compositions without depth
- Use photographic or digitally rendered imagery
- Apply blurry, noisy, or deformed textures
- Include painting or drawing-style elements
- Make heavy, dense compositions without breathing room

## Best For

Craft and handmade brand presentations, wedding and event planning, greeting card design showcases, fine art exhibitions, decorative arts workshops, artisan product launches
