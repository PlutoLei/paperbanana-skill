# Baroque

Dramatic opulence with rich ornamentation, deep contrasts, and theatrical grandeur

## Design Aesthetic

Lavish and theatrical visual language inspired by 17th-century Baroque art. Deep chiaroscuro contrasts with rich, saturated colors and elaborate ornamental details. Every element conveys exuberance, movement, and emotional intensity, creating a sense of awe and magnificence.

## Background

- Color: Deep Burgundy (#2C0A16) or Rich Midnight Blue (#0D1B2A)
- Texture: Aged oil canvas texture with subtle craquelure and gold leaf accents

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Ornate serif with high contrast strokes and decorative swashes. Blackletter or Didone-style capitals with dramatic thick-thin variation. Commanding presence evoking historical grandeur.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Classical old-style serif with elegant proportions. Garamond or Caslon-inspired letterforms. Warm and readable with traditional character.

## Visual Elements

- Ornate gilded frames and scrollwork borders
- Chiaroscuro lighting with dramatic shadows
- Cherub and floral motifs as decorative accents
- Rich velvet and drapery textures
- Classical columns and architectural elements
- Elaborate cartouche title panels
- Dynamic diagonal compositions suggesting movement
- Gold leaf overlays on key focal points

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use dramatic light-dark contrast in every composition
- Layer rich textures and ornamental details generously
- Employ dynamic, diagonal compositions
- Include gold and metallic accents for opulence
- Create depth through overlapping elements and shadows

### Don't

- Use flat, minimalist layouts
- Apply bright neon or pastel colors
- Leave large areas of empty white space
- Use modern sans-serif typography
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Art history presentations, museum exhibitions, luxury brand storytelling, classical music programs, theatrical event materials, historical lectures, fine dining menus
