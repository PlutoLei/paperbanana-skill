# Blueprint Schematic Drawing

Technical blueprint aesthetic with white-on-blue schematics, grid lines, and engineering precision

## Design Aesthetic

Precise technical drawing style inspired by architectural and engineering blueprints. White linework on deep blue backgrounds with fine grid overlays. Callouts, dimension lines, and annotation markers give every slide the feel of a professional schematic. Clean, authoritative, and information-dense.

## Background

- Color: Blueprint Blue (#003366) or Deep Navy (#0A1A3A)
- Texture: Fine grid overlay with subtle paper grain, cyanotype-inspired wash

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Monospaced or technical sans-serif in uppercase. Reminiscent of engineering stencil lettering. Crisp, uniform strokes with no decorative flourishes.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Light-weight monospaced or narrow sans-serif. Highly legible at small sizes for annotation-style text. Consistent character width for alignment.

## Visual Elements

- Fine grid background with major and minor divisions
- Dimension lines with arrowheads and measurements
- Callout bubbles with leader lines
- Section markers and detail zoom circles
- Dashed lines for hidden or projected elements
- Title blocks in lower-right corners
- Revision clouds around modified areas
- Cross-section hatching for filled areas

## Style Rules

### Do

- Maintain consistent line weights (thin for grid, medium for elements, thick for emphasis)
- Use technical annotation conventions (leaders, callouts, dimensions)
- Keep compositions precise and grid-aligned
- Include schematic symbols and notation
- Let the grid structure guide all placement

### Don't

- Use color fills or gradients
- Include photographs or organic shapes
- Apply drop shadows or glow effects
- Use decorative or script typefaces
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Engineering presentations, architecture reviews, technical product overviews, patent illustrations, manufacturing processes, system design walkthroughs, hardware documentation
