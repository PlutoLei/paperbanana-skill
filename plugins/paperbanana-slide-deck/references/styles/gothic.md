# Gothic

Dark, mysterious aesthetic with dramatic ornate detailing and haunting atmosphere

## Design Aesthetic

Brooding and atmospheric compositions drawn from Gothic architecture and Victorian dark romanticism. Deep shadows, dramatic contrasts, and intricate ornamental patterns create a sense of mystery and grandeur. Every element carries weight and emotional intensity.

## Background

- Color: Obsidian Black (#1A1A2E)
- Texture: Dark stone or aged velvet texture with subtle candlelight gradient at edges

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Cinzel Decorative or similar Gothic-inspired serif. Strong, dramatic letterforms with sharp serifs and high contrast strokes. Commanding and ominous presence.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Cormorant Garamond or similar refined serif for body text. Elegant with slight old-world character. High readability against dark backgrounds.

## Visual Elements

- Pointed arch frames inspired by Gothic cathedrals
- Intricate filigree and ornamental borders
- Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting with deep shadows
- Rose window and tracery-inspired decorative patterns
- Candelabra and flame motifs for section dividers
- Gargoyle or grotesque silhouette accents
- Thorny vine and wrought iron decorative elements
- Cross-hatching textures in illustration areas

## Style Rules

### Do

- Maintain high contrast between light and dark elements
- Use ornate, intricate decorative details
- Create atmospheric depth with layered shadows
- Keep the mood consistently dark and dramatic
- Use vertical emphasis reflecting Gothic architecture

### Don't

- Use bright, cheerful colors or pastels
- Include modern minimalist elements
- Add cartoonish or lighthearted imagery
- Use rounded, bubbly shapes
- Include optimistic or whimsical flourishes

## Best For

Horror and dark fiction presentations, historical architecture talks, literature analysis, music and arts showcases, Halloween-themed content, dark brand storytelling
