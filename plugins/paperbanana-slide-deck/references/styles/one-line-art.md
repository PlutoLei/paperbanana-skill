# One Line Art

Continuous single-line drawings with minimalist elegance on clean white backgrounds

## Design Aesthetic

The ultimate exercise in economy -- every subject is rendered with a single continuous unbroken line that never lifts from the surface. Inspired by Picasso's one-line drawings and modern continuous-line portraiture, the style achieves remarkable expressiveness through radical simplicity. The line flows, curves, and doubles back on itself to define form, space, and even emotion with nothing but its own path.

## Background

- Color: Pure White (#FFFFFF)
- Texture: None -- pristine white to let the single line command all attention

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Didact Gothic or similar thin geometric sans-serif. Single-weight strokes that echo the continuous line aesthetic. Minimal and precise.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Work Sans Light or similar thin sans-serif. Airy and unobtrusive. Lets the line art remain the visual focus.

## Visual Elements

- Single continuous unbroken line forming subjects
- Line weight variation from pen pressure
- Flowing curves defining organic forms
- Negative space as active compositional element
- Minimal use of space -- concentrated compositions
- Abstract representations of recognizable subjects
- Elegant loops and crossings where the line overlaps
- Symbolic reduction to essential contours

## Style Rules

### Do

- Keep the line truly continuous and unbroken
- Use a single consistent stroke weight (with natural variation)
- Let negative space do the heavy lifting
- Reduce subjects to their most essential contours
- Maintain clean white backgrounds

### Don't

- Add shading, fills, or color blocks
- Use multiple disconnected lines
- Include detailed internal features
- Add decorative elements beyond the line itself
- Use complex or busy backgrounds

## Best For

Minimalist portfolios, modern art presentations, branding showcases, meditation and mindfulness content, elegant invitations, contemporary design talks
