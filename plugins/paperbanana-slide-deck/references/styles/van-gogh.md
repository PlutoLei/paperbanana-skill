# Van Gogh

Expressive oil painting style with bold impasto brushwork, swirling textures, and vibrant color

## Design Aesthetic

Channels the unmistakable energy of Vincent van Gogh -- thick impasto brushstrokes that swirl and dance across the surface, intense color applied with raw emotional force, and compositions that pulse with movement. Every element is rendered with visible, directional brushwork that makes the paint itself a subject. The world is seen through heightened perception where stars spiral, fields undulate, and skies burn.

## Background

- Color: Night Blue (#1E3A5F) or Wheat Gold (#E8B84B)
- Texture: Heavy impasto paint surface with visible raised brushstrokes

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Merriweather Bold or similar robust serif. Strong presence that can hold its own against intense painterly backgrounds. Warm and expressive.

### Secondary Font (Body)

PT Serif or similar readable serif. Regular weight with classical proportions. Grounded and legible against textured backgrounds.

## Visual Elements

- Bold directional brushstrokes following contours
- Swirling spiral patterns in skies and backgrounds
- Thick impasto texture with visible paint ridges
- Intense complementary color contrasts
- Strong outlines around forms (cloisonnist influence)
- Rhythmic repetition of brushstroke patterns
- Expressive distortion of natural forms
- Luminous color creating inner light

## Style Rules

### Do

- Make every brushstroke visible and directional
- Use bold complementary color contrasts
- Apply swirling movement to backgrounds and skies
- Let emotion drive color intensity choices
- Maintain textural richness across the entire surface

### Don't

- Use smooth gradients or flat fills
- Include realistic photographic elements
- Apply calm, static compositions
- Use muted or corporate color palettes
- Add text, watermarks, or frames to imagery

## Best For

Art appreciation talks, creative expression workshops, mental health awareness, nature presentations, cultural lectures, expressive storytelling
