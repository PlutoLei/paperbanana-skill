# Color Sketchnote

Vibrant hand-drawn visual notes with playful illustrations, icons, and dynamic typography

## Design Aesthetic

A colorful evolution of visual note-taking, combining hand-drawn lettering, quick illustrations, graphic icons, and dynamic layouts into engaging visual narratives. Unlike monochrome sketchnotes, this style explodes with color -- using it to create hierarchy, group related ideas, and inject energy. The layout feels organic and personal, as if captured live during an inspiring talk.

## Background

- Color: Notebook White (#FEFEFE)
- Texture: Subtle dot grid or faint ruled lines like a premium notebook

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Permanent Marker or similar hand-lettered display font. Bold, energetic, slightly irregular. Feels drawn with a thick marker in real time.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Patrick Hand or similar casual handwriting font. Legible and warm, as if written quickly but carefully.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Background | Notebook White | #FEFEFE | Primary background |
| Primary | Marker Blue | #2563EB | Main headings, key icons |

## Visual Elements

- Hand-lettered headers with varied sizes and styles
- Quick sketch icons and spot illustrations
- Connecting arrows and flow lines
- Highlight boxes and speech bubbles
- Banner ribbons for section headers
- Numbered lists with hand-drawn bullets
- Color-coded grouping of related concepts
- Doodle borders and decorative separators

## Style Rules

### Do

- Mix typography styles and sizes dynamically
- Use color to create visual hierarchy and grouping
- Include quick illustrative icons for key concepts
- Connect ideas visually with arrows and lines
- Maintain an energetic, spontaneous feel

### Don't

- Use rigid grid-based layouts
- Apply photographic or realistic imagery
- Make typography uniform and mechanical
- Use dark or muted color schemes
- Create overly polished or corporate aesthetics

## Best For

Conference recaps, workshop summaries, brainstorming sessions, educational content, meeting notes, knowledge sharing presentations
