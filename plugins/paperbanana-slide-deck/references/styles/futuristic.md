# Futuristic

Sleek, ultramodern style with high-tech minimalism and cutting-edge design

## Design Aesthetic

Pristine and forward-looking visual language that feels like it was designed in 2050. Ultra-clean surfaces, precise geometry, and luminous accents create an atmosphere of advanced technology and innovation. Every element communicates progress, efficiency, and the promise of tomorrow.

## Background

- Color: Near-White (#F8F9FA) or Deep Space (#0F1923)
- Texture: Smooth gradient with subtle metallic sheen or frosted glass effect

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Ultra-modern geometric sans-serif like Outfit, Space Grotesk, or Sora. Clean mathematical proportions with futuristic character. Thin to medium weights.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Technical sans-serif like Inter or IBM Plex Sans. Optimized for screen readability. Precision-engineered letterforms.

## Visual Elements

- Frosted glass (glassmorphism) cards and panels
- Thin luminous line illustrations
- Floating 3D geometric objects
- Subtle gradient orbs and light sources
- Data visualization with glowing nodes
- Clean wireframe and blueprint elements
- Smooth curved UI-inspired shapes
- Holographic shimmer effects

## Style Rules

### Do

- Maintain extreme cleanliness and precision
- Use luminous accents on clean backgrounds
- Create depth through layered translucent surfaces
- Keep shapes geometric and mathematically precise
- Use generous whitespace as a design element

### Don't

- Use organic, hand-drawn, or rough textures
- Apply vintage, retro, or aged effects
- Create visually heavy or cluttered compositions
- Use warm earth tones or rustic colors
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Technology product launches, AI and machine learning presentations, startup pitch decks, innovation strategy talks, SaaS product demos, scientific research overviews, forward-looking corporate keynotes
