# Cyberpunk Cityscape

Neon-lit futuristic urban style with dark alleys, towering skyscrapers, and high-contrast vibrancy

## Design Aesthetic

Gritty and electrifying dystopian urban landscape where technology and decay coexist. Towering holographic billboards illuminate rain-soaked streets in neon pink and cyan. Dark atmospheric backgrounds punctuated by brilliant light sources create a cinematic noir-meets-future world of high contrast and visual intensity.

## Background

- Color: Deep Void Black (#0A0A14) or Dark Cyber Blue (#0D1117)
- Texture: Rain-streaked glass, wet asphalt reflections, or digital noise grain

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Futuristic tech display like Orbitron, Rajdhani Bold, or Blade Runner-inspired fonts. Sharp angular forms with neon glow effects. Digital yet edgy.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Monospace or tech sans-serif like JetBrains Mono or Exo 2. Terminal-style readability with futuristic undertone.

## Visual Elements

- Neon sign and holographic billboard elements
- Rain-soaked reflective surfaces
- Towering cityscape silhouettes
- Glitch and scan-line effects
- Holographic UI panel overlays
- Japanese/CJK neon text decorations
- Circuit board and data stream patterns
- Atmospheric fog and volumetric light beams

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use dark backgrounds with neon accent lighting
- Create depth through atmospheric fog layers
- Apply glow effects to neon-colored elements
- Use high contrast between dark and light
- Layer visual elements for urban density

### Don't

- Use natural or pastoral imagery
- Create bright, well-lit daytime scenes
- Apply muted or earth-tone palettes
- Use traditional serif or handwritten fonts
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Tech startup pitches, gaming industry presentations, sci-fi world-building, VR/AR technology showcases, electronic music events, urban futurism talks, cybersecurity presentations
