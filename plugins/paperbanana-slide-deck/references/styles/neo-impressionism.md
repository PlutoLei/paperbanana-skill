# Neo-Impressionism

Pointillist painting style with tiny dabs of color creating luminous, painterly compositions

## Design Aesthetic

Inspired by Seurat and Signac, compositions are built from myriad tiny dots and dabs of pure color that optically blend at distance. The result is luminous, shimmering, and unmistakably hand-crafted. Every surface vibrates with chromatic energy while maintaining compositional harmony.

## Background

- Color: Warm Canvas (#FDF6EC)
- Texture: Visible paper grain with subtle dot-pattern undertone

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Playfair Display or similar high-contrast serif. Regular to semibold weight. Elegant and art-historical, complementing the painterly aesthetic.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Source Serif Pro or similar readable serif. Clean letterforms that feel editorial and cultured without competing with the visual texture.

## Visual Elements

- Dense pointillist dot patterns forming imagery
- Visible individual color dabs and brushstrokes
- Soft edges where colors optically blend
- Luminous light effects through pure color juxtaposition
- Natural subjects rendered in painterly abstraction
- Color halos and atmospheric diffusion
- Complementary color pairs placed side by side

## Style Rules

### Do

- Build imagery from small discrete color marks
- Use complementary color pairs for vibrancy
- Maintain a luminous, light-filled quality
- Keep compositions balanced with clear focal points
- Allow painterly texture to be visible and celebrated

### Don't

- Use flat solid fills or gradients
- Include photographic or realistic elements
- Apply sharp, crisp vector edges
- Use dark moody palettes
- Add modern UI elements or icons

## Best For

Art history presentations, creative workshops, gallery exhibitions, cultural events, nature-themed talks, education on color theory
