# Zentangle

Intricate meditative pattern art with abstract monochrome designs and hypnotic detail

## Design Aesthetic

Mesmerizing compositions built from repetitive, structured patterns that fill every surface with intricate linework. Meditative and abstract, the style transforms simple geometric and organic motifs into complex, harmonious wholes. The monochrome palette keeps focus on pattern and rhythm.

## Background

- Color: Warm White (#FAF9F6)
- Texture: Smooth drawing paper with subtle tooth, no digital noise

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Didot or similar high-contrast serif. Elegant thin-thick stroke contrast mirrors the fine linework of zentangle patterns. Refined and contemplative.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Lato or similar clean sans-serif for body text. Provides contrast against the ornate patterns. Clear and neutral to let the art breathe.

## Visual Elements

- Dense repeating geometric patterns (grids, spirals, waves)
- Organic flowing motifs (feathers, leaves, tendrils)
- Progressive complexity from border to center
- Fine pen-stroke linework at consistent weight
- Shading through hatching and stippling
- Pattern-filled letterforms and shapes
- Mandala-inspired circular compositions
- Border patterns framing content areas

## Style Rules

### Do

- Fill spaces with intricate, structured patterns
- Maintain consistent line weight throughout
- Use pattern density to create visual hierarchy
- Keep the palette monochrome or near-monochrome
- Create meditative, rhythmic visual flow

### Don't

- Use colorful, vibrant multi-color fills
- Include representational or realistic imagery
- Create simplistic layouts with large empty areas
- Use large solid color fields without pattern
- Add photographic or digital texture overlays

## Best For

Mindfulness and wellness presentations, art technique workshops, creative process talks, abstract concept visualization, meditation and focus-themed content
