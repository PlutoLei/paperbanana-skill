# Real Estate

Professional real estate photography style with inviting, well-lit compositions

## Design Aesthetic

Bright, airy, and welcoming visual style that showcases spaces at their best. Clean layouts with professional photography as the centerpiece. Warm natural light and open compositions create an aspirational yet approachable feel that makes properties feel like home.

## Background

- Color: Clean White (#FAFAFA) or Light Warm Gray (#F0EDEA)
- Texture: Smooth matte finish, subtle architectural line patterns

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Modern sans-serif like Raleway or Poppins. Clean and professional with moderate weight. Approachable yet authoritative.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Neutral sans-serif like Open Sans or Source Sans Pro. Highly readable at all sizes. Professional and trustworthy.

## Visual Elements

- Full-bleed architectural photography with natural light
- Clean grid layouts with image galleries
- Subtle shadow cards for property info
- Map and location markers
- Before/after comparison frames
- Thin line icons for amenities and features
- Warm gradient overlays on hero images
- Statistical callouts with large numbers

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use well-lit, wide-angle property photography
- Maintain clean, grid-based layouts
- Highlight key metrics prominently (sqft, price, rooms)
- Use warm, inviting color tones
- Keep text concise and scannable

### Don't

- Use dark or moody backgrounds
- Overcrowd slides with text descriptions
- Use artistic filters on property photos
- Include cluttered or poorly staged imagery
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Property listings, real estate marketing decks, development proposals, neighborhood guides, open house presentations, investment property analysis, interior design showcases
