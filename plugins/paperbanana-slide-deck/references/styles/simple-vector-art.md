# Simple Vector Art

Clean 2D vector graphics with simple geometric shapes, flat color, and professional minimalism

## Design Aesthetic

Distilled to essentials, this style uses simple geometric shapes and flat color fills to communicate clearly and professionally. Every element is reduced to its most basic vector form -- circles, rectangles, triangles -- arranged with deliberate spacing. The result is crisp, scalable, and universally readable, like premium iconography expanded to full compositions.

## Background

- Color: Clean White (#FAFAFA) or Soft Gray (#F1F5F9)
- Texture: None -- perfectly flat and clean

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Poppins Semibold or similar rounded geometric sans-serif. Friendly yet professional. Clean curves that match the vector aesthetic.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Nunito Sans or similar soft sans-serif. Regular weight for comfortable reading. Approachable and modern.

## Visual Elements

- Simple geometric shapes (circles, rounded rectangles)
- Flat color fills with no gradients or shadows
- Consistent stroke widths when outlines are used
- Icon-style illustrations at larger scale
- Generous white space between elements
- Clean alignment on invisible grid
- Minimal line-art accents
- Two-tone or three-tone color combinations per element

## Style Rules

### Do

- Reduce every visual to its simplest geometric form
- Maintain consistent proportions and spacing
- Use a limited subset of the palette per slide
- Keep all edges crisp and mathematically precise
- Ensure every element serves a communication purpose

### Don't

- Add realistic detail, textures, or gradients
- Use photographic or 3D elements
- Apply drop shadows or glow effects
- Create complex multi-layered illustrations
- Use more than 3-4 colors in a single composition

## Best For

Product explainers, onboarding decks, SaaS presentations, infographic summaries, mobile app showcases, instructional materials
