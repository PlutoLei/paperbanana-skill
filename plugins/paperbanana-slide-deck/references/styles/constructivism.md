# Constructivism

Bold geometric abstraction with industrial aesthetics and revolutionary dynamic compositions

## Design Aesthetic

Inspired by the Russian Constructivist movement of the 1920s. Geometric abstraction, bold diagonals, and photomontage-influenced layouts create a sense of industrial power and ideological urgency. High-contrast color blocks and angular compositions convey dynamic energy and utilitarian purpose.

## Background

- Color: Off-White (#F0EDE5) or Industrial Cream (#E8E2D4)
- Texture: Rough newsprint or lithograph texture with visible fiber

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Heavy condensed sans-serif or slab face, often set on steep diagonals. Bold, block-like letterforms with strong geometric character. Frequently all-caps with dramatic scale contrast.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Sturdy sans-serif at medium weight. Functional and legible, reflecting industrial utility. Often set in structured columns.

## Visual Elements

- Bold diagonal lines and wedge shapes driving composition
- Angular geometric forms: triangles, parallelograms, arrow shapes
- High-contrast photomontage-style layering
- Thick ruled bars separating content zones
- Circular badge and gear motifs representing industry
- Large-scale numerals and typographic elements as visual anchors
- Red and black as dominant graphic forces
- Overlapping planes creating depth and tension

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use strong diagonals and angular compositions
- Maintain high contrast between elements
- Embrace bold, utilitarian graphic forms
- Keep the color palette restricted to red, black, gray, and gold
- Create a sense of dynamic tension and movement

### Don't

- Use organic, curved, or ornate shapes
- Apply muted, pastel, or subtle color palettes
- Include traditional or decorative ornamentation
- Create static, symmetrical layouts
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Political and social commentary, industrial design history, propaganda art studies, revolutionary movements, design education, bold brand manifestos, activist presentations
