# Knolling Photography

Perfectly organized flat-lay arrangements with objects aligned at right angles on clean surfaces

## Design Aesthetic

Meticulous overhead photography style where objects are arranged in precise parallel and perpendicular alignments. Every item is carefully curated, cleaned, and positioned at exact 90-degree angles to create satisfying visual order. The bird's-eye perspective and obsessive organization transform ordinary objects into compelling visual inventories. Clean, systematic, and deeply satisfying.

## Background

- Color: Clean White (#FFFFFF) or Warm Concrete (#E8E4DE)
- Texture: Smooth matte surface, clean workbench, or minimal paper backdrop

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Clean geometric sans-serif with precise proportions. Structured and orderly to match the systematic visual approach. Medium to bold weight with exact alignment.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Monospaced or tabular sans-serif for catalog-style labeling. Precise character widths for clean column alignment. Functional and inventory-like.

## Visual Elements

- Overhead bird's-eye camera perspective exclusively
- Objects aligned in strict parallel/perpendicular grid
- Generous equal spacing between all items
- Soft even lighting with minimal shadows
- Clean labeled annotations pointing to objects
- Grouped items by category or color
- Deconstructed product breakdowns (every-part-visible)
- Precise margins and uniform spacing throughout

## Style Rules

### Do

- Align every object at exact right angles to frame edges
- Use consistent spacing between all elements
- Shoot from directly overhead with even lighting
- Group objects logically by type, color, or size
- Maintain a clean, uncluttered surface

### Don't

- Place objects at random angles
- Use dramatic shadows or directional lighting
- Include cluttered or overlapping arrangements
- Apply filters that obscure object clarity
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Product inventory showcases, tool and equipment catalogs, recipe ingredient displays, EDC (everyday carry) features, manufacturing process breakdowns, retail merchandising, organized workspace presentations
