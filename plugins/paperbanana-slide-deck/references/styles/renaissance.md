# Renaissance

Classical realistic style with perspective, dramatic lighting, and timeless grandeur

## Design Aesthetic

Majestic and scholarly visual language inspired by the Italian Renaissance masters. Rich chiaroscuro lighting, mathematical perspective, and classical composition create an atmosphere of timeless authority. Every element references the golden age of art and learning, conveying gravitas and intellectual depth.

## Background

- Color: Aged Parchment (#E8DCC8) or Dark Venetian (#2C1A1A)
- Texture: Cracked fresco or aged vellum with patina

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Classical Roman serif like Trajan Pro or Cinzel. Carved-in-stone authority with elegant proportions inspired by Roman inscriptions.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Traditional book serif like Garamond or Palatino. Perfectly suited for extended reading. Centuries of refinement in every letterform.

## Visual Elements

- Classical architectural frames (columns, arches, pediments)
- Gilded ornamental borders and corner decorations
- Chiaroscuro dramatic lighting effects
- Religious and mythological iconographic references
- Perspective grids and vanishing point compositions
- Scroll and cartouche text frames
- Cherub and classical figure decorative accents
- Trompe l'oeil dimensional effects

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use classical symmetrical composition
- Apply dramatic light-to-dark value contrasts
- Incorporate gilded and ornamental decorative frames
- Maintain a warm, aged earth-tone palette
- Reference classical proportion and perspective

### Don't

- Use modern flat design or minimalism
- Apply bright neon or artificial colors
- Create casual or informal layouts
- Use sans-serif or modern display typefaces
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Art history presentations, museum and cultural heritage talks, academic lectures, luxury brand heritage stories, classical music programs, architecture and restoration projects, scholarly conferences
