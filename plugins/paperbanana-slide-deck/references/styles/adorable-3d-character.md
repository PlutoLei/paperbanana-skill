# Adorable 3D Character

Cute 3D rendered character art with charming proportions, soft materials, and playful scenes

## Design Aesthetic

Warm and delightful compositions featuring lovable 3D characters with exaggerated cute proportions. Soft, pillowy materials, rounded forms, and gentle studio lighting create an inviting world that feels like a premium animated film. Characters and objects have a tactile quality suggesting clay, vinyl, or soft plastic.

## Background

- Color: Soft Lavender (#F3E5F5)
- Texture: Smooth pastel gradient with subtle ambient occlusion suggesting a 3D studio environment

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Fredoka One or similar bold rounded sans-serif. Chunky, bubbly letterforms that match the 3D character aesthetic. Friendly and immediately appealing.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Varela Round or similar rounded sans-serif for body text. Soft and approachable at all sizes. Maintains the 3D character world consistency.

## Visual Elements

- Rounded, pillow-soft 3D character forms
- Oversized heads with large expressive eyes
- Soft studio lighting with gentle shadows
- Smooth, toylike material surfaces
- Pastel-colored 3D environments
- Tiny hands and feet with exaggerated proportions
- Subtle subsurface scattering on character skin
- Cheerful props and accessories in scene

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use soft, rounded forms for all 3D elements
- Apply gentle, diffused studio lighting
- Create characters with adorable proportions
- Maintain a warm, inviting pastel environment
- Show tactile material quality (clay, vinyl, plush)

### Don't

- Include photographic or realistic rendering
- Use dark, grungy, or sloppy textures
- Create ugly, deformed, or uncanny characters
- Apply noisy, blurry, or low-quality rendering
- Use flat 2D illustration style

## Best For

Children's product presentations, animation studio pitches, game character showcases, toy and merchandise design, educational app demos, family-friendly brand content
