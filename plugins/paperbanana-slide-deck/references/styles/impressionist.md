# Impressionist

Loose brushwork painting style capturing light, color, and atmospheric feeling

## Design Aesthetic

Soft and luminous visual language inspired by Monet, Renoir, and the Impressionist masters. Loose visible brushwork captures the fleeting quality of light and atmosphere rather than precise detail. Colors blend optically, scenes shimmer with natural light, and every composition feels like a moment caught in passing.

## Background

- Color: Soft Linen (#F5F0E8) or Morning Mist (#E8E4DF)
- Texture: Visible canvas weave with soft painted edges

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Elegant serif with painterly character like Playfair Display or Cormorant. Refined but not rigid, with natural grace that echoes artistic sensibility.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Soft rounded sans-serif like Nunito or Quicksand. Gentle and approachable, like handwriting translated to type.

## Visual Elements

- Soft dappled light effects across compositions
- Visible brush strokes as texture in all painted elements
- Blurred edges suggesting movement and atmosphere
- Garden and landscape scene backgrounds
- Water reflection effects with broken color
- Floral and botanical illustration accents
- Soft color-blending transitions between sections
- Light-filled compositions with bright highlights

## Style Rules

### Do

- Capture the quality of light and atmosphere
- Use loose, visible brush strokes in all artwork
- Blend colors optically rather than mixing flat
- Create soft edges and gentle transitions
- Convey mood and feeling over precise detail

### Don't

- Use sharp geometric shapes or hard edges
- Apply flat digital color fills
- Create dark, heavy, or gloomy palettes
- Use photographic precision or hyperrealism
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Art education talks, travel and destination presentations, wellness and mindfulness content, wine and culinary events, garden and landscape showcases, seasonal campaign materials, cultural institution presentations
