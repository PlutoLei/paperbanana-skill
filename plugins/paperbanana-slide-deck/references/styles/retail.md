# Retail

Vibrant retail packaging style with enticing, eye-catching commercial appeal

## Design Aesthetic

Bold and energetic visual language designed to grab attention and drive action. Vibrant colors and dynamic compositions create shelf-appeal energy. Product-focused layouts with punchy graphics that translate the excitement of retail discovery into presentation form.

## Background

- Color: Bright White (#FFFFFF) or Soft Yellow (#FFF9E0)
- Texture: Clean with subtle dot pattern or halftone overlay

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Bold condensed sans-serif like Impact, Oswald Bold, or Bebas Neue. High-impact display type with tight tracking. Commands attention immediately.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Friendly rounded sans-serif like Nunito or Quicksand. Approachable and easy to read. Maintains commercial energy.

## Visual Elements

- Product photography with clean white backgrounds
- Bold price callouts and promotional badges
- Starburst and banner shapes for deals
- Before/after or comparison layouts
- Color-blocked section dividers
- Shopping-inspired iconography (cart, bag, tags)
- Grid-based product showcases
- Testimonial or review star ratings

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use bold, saturated colors for energy
- Make key information (price, features) immediately visible
- Create clear visual hierarchy with scale contrast
- Use product imagery as hero elements
- Include clear calls-to-action

### Don't

- Use muted or pastel color palettes
- Bury important details in body text
- Create layouts without a clear focal point
- Use overly artistic or abstract imagery
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Product launches, retail marketing decks, e-commerce presentations, promotional campaigns, packaging design reviews, trade show materials, sales team training
