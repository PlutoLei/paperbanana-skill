# Futurism

Dynamic speed and energy with angular motion lines, fragmented forms, and technological optimism

## Design Aesthetic

Explosive visual energy inspired by the Italian Futurist movement's obsession with speed, technology, and dynamism. Compositions shatter into angular fragments suggesting rapid motion. Overlapping forms, force lines, and radiating diagonals create a sense of perpetual acceleration. Bold, aggressive, and forward-looking.

## Background

- Color: Deep Gunmetal (#1C1C1E) or Dark Navy (#0D1321)
- Texture: Metallic sheen with subtle motion blur and radiating line patterns

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Bold italic sans-serif or custom angular lettering. Slanted heavily to suggest forward motion. Condensed and sharp, with aggressive proportions that cut across the composition.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Condensed sans-serif in bold weight. Italicized or oblique setting for kinetic energy. Tight leading and letter-spacing to maintain velocity.

## Visual Elements

- Speed lines and force vectors radiating from focal points
- Fragmented overlapping forms suggesting simultaneous motion
- Angular shattered-glass compositions
- Radiating diagonal lines creating depth and movement
- Machine and gear silhouettes as decorative motifs
- Metallic and chrome reflective surfaces
- Spinning wheel and propeller abstractions
- Stroboscopic repetition of forms showing movement phases

## Style Rules

### Do

- Fill compositions with directional energy and motion
- Use sharp diagonals and radiating lines aggressively
- Fragment forms to suggest speed and dynamism
- Maintain a dark, high-contrast metallic palette
- Overlap elements to create kinetic tension

### Don't

- Use static, centered, symmetrical layouts
- Apply soft organic curves or gentle gradients
- Create calm or restful compositions
- Use pastel or muted color palettes
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Automotive and transportation presentations, technology launches, sports and athletics content, innovation keynotes, robotics and engineering demos, startup vision presentations, gaming industry talks
