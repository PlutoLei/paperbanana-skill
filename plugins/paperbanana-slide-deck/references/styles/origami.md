# Origami

Paper-folded art with pleated surfaces, crisp creases, and elegant sculptural simplicity

## Design Aesthetic

The art of paper folding translated into a visual style. Every element appears sculpted from a single sheet through precise cuts and folds. Crisp creases, triangulated surfaces, and pleated geometries create elegant three-dimensional forms. The aesthetic is clean, meditative, and structurally beautiful, celebrating the transformation of flat material into sculptural wonder.

## Background

- Color: Soft Paper White (#F5F2ED) or Light Cream (#FAF7F2)
- Texture: Clean paper surface with subtle fiber and very faint shadow

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Clean geometric sans-serif with angular, folded character. Letterforms that suggest paper-fold construction. Medium to bold weight with precise, sharp edges.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Light-weight sans-serif. Minimal and paper-clean. Complements the origami aesthetic without adding visual weight.

## Visual Elements

- Crisp paper folds with precise geometric creases
- Triangulated surfaces with flat-shaded facets
- Origami animals, flowers, and geometric forms as illustrations
- Subtle directional shadows revealing fold depth
- Clean centered compositions on paper-like backgrounds
- Pleated and layered paper textures
- Minimal props: the paper form is the star
- Gentle gradient across folded surfaces suggesting light

## Style Rules

### Do

- Make all elements appear constructed from folded paper
- Use crisp, clean creases and precise geometry
- Center compositions with generous whitespace
- Apply subtle shadows to reveal three-dimensional folds
- Maintain elegant simplicity in all sculptural forms

### Don't

- Use noisy, sloppy, messy, or grainy textures
- Create highly detailed or ultra-textured surfaces
- Apply photographic or realistic material rendering
- Include unrelated non-paper elements
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Design process presentations, Japanese culture content, craft and maker workshops, minimalist product showcases, mathematics and geometry education, meditation and mindfulness talks, paper engineering demonstrations
