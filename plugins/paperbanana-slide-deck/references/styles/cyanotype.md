# Cyanotype

Prussian blue photographic print aesthetic with high contrast, blueprint tones, and handmade experimental quality

## Design Aesthetic

The world rendered exclusively in shades of Prussian blue and white, as if every image were a sun-printed cyanotype on treated paper. This historic photographic process produces haunting, high-contrast imagery with distinctive blue tonality. Silhouette effects, delicate botanical detail, and the unmistakable handmade quality of sun exposure create an atmosphere that is simultaneously scientific and deeply artistic.

## Background

- Color: Cyanotype Blue (#0A3D6B)
- Texture: Visible paper fiber and chemical wash edges, uneven coating marks

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Spectral or similar refined serif. Light to regular weight. Elegant and slightly scientific, matching the process's origins in photographic chemistry.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Fira Sans Light or similar clean sans-serif. High readability against blue backgrounds. Technical but warm.

## Visual Elements

- Monochromatic blue tonal range exclusively
- High-contrast silhouette effects
- Botanical specimens and nature impressions
- Visible paper edges and coating boundaries
- Sun-bleached highlight areas
- Chemical wash marks and uneven coating
- Contact-print sharpness in foreground elements
- Soft atmospheric fade in backgrounds

## Style Rules

### Do

- Restrict palette strictly to blue-and-white tonal range
- Include visible handmade process artifacts
- Use high contrast between deep blue and bright white
- Show paper edges and coating irregularities
- Maintain the photographic contact-print quality

### Don't

- Introduce colors outside the blue tonal range
- Create clean digital-looking edges
- Use flat uniform backgrounds
- Apply modern graphic design elements
- Make compositions look digitally processed

## Best For

Botanical presentations, photography history talks, alternative process workshops, science and chemistry lectures, nature documentation, art school exhibitions
