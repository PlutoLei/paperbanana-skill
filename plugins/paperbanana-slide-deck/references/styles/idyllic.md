# Idyllic

Peaceful pastoral beauty with warm light, soft colors, and harmonious natural serenity

## Design Aesthetic

Gentle and dreamy visual language that evokes an idealized countryside or garden setting. Soft golden-hour lighting bathes every composition in warmth. Pastel tones, flowing organic shapes, and abundant natural motifs create a sense of blissful tranquility. The overall effect is calming, optimistic, and picturesque.

## Background

- Color: Warm Cream (#FFF9F0) or Soft Sky (#E8F0FE)
- Texture: Soft watercolor wash or gentle linen canvas with warm light bleed

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Elegant script or soft serif with graceful curves. Flowing letterforms that echo natural organic shapes. Warm and inviting with a handwritten or calligraphic quality.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Light-weight rounded sans-serif with open counters. Gentle and easy to read with a friendly, approachable personality. Matches the serene mood without demanding attention.

## Visual Elements

- Soft floral borders and botanical corner accents
- Golden-hour light gradients and warm lens flares
- Rolling hills and meadow landscape silhouettes
- Butterflies, birds, and gentle nature creatures
- Flowing ribbon banners for section titles
- Soft cloud shapes as text containers
- Pastel watercolor washes behind content areas
- Delicate vine and leaf divider lines

## Style Rules

### Do

- Maintain a warm, golden-hour lighting feel throughout
- Use soft organic shapes with no hard angles
- Keep colors pastel and harmoniously blended
- Include abundant nature-inspired decorative elements
- Create a sense of gentle movement through flowing forms

### Don't

- Use dark, moody, or high-contrast palettes
- Apply sharp geometric shapes or rigid grids
- Include urban, industrial, or technological imagery
- Create cluttered or visually intense compositions
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Wedding and event planning, wellness and self-care content, travel destination showcases, children's storytelling, garden and lifestyle topics, inspirational and motivational talks, hospitality brand presentations
