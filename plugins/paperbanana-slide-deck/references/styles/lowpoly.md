# Low Poly

Faceted low-polygon game art with sharp geometric surfaces and retro 3D charm

## Design Aesthetic

Celebrates the faceted beauty of low-polygon 3D art. Simple polygon meshes with flat-shaded triangular faces create a distinctively angular, geometric world. Jagged edges and blocky forms carry the nostalgic charm of early 3D gaming while feeling fresh and stylized. Wireframe hints and centered compositions reinforce the geometric construction.

## Background

- Color: Gradient Sky (#87CEEB to #E0F0FF) or Flat Mint (#E0F5F0)
- Texture: Clean and flat with subtle polygon-edge highlight

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Angular geometric sans-serif with sharp terminals. Bold weight, slightly condensed. Letterforms echo the faceted quality of low-poly geometry. Modern and game-inspired.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean geometric sans-serif at regular weight. Readable and straightforward, complementing the angular visual style.

## Visual Elements

- Flat-shaded triangular polygon faces with distinct edges
- Low vertex count creating blocky, angular forms
- Visible wireframe edges on select elements
- Simple terrain with triangulated landscape geometry
- Centered composition with clear focal subjects
- Gradient shading per-face creating dimensionality
- Miniature low-poly trees, buildings, and characters
- Subtle ambient shadow beneath objects

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use flat-shaded triangular polygon faces consistently
- Keep polygon count visibly low with jagged, blocky forms
- Center compositions with clear geometric subjects
- Apply per-face color variation for depth
- Maintain the clean, stylized game-art aesthetic

### Don't

- Use highly detailed, ultra-textured, or smooth surfaces
- Apply noisy, sloppy, messy, or grainy textures
- Create photographic or realistic rendering
- Smooth out polygon edges or use subdivision surfaces
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Game design presentations, 3D modeling education, indie game showcases, geometric art discussions, tech and startup product demos, environmental visualization, playful data representation
