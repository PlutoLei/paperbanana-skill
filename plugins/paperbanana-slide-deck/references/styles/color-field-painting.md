# Color Field Painting

Large expanses of saturated color with minimal form, evoking contemplative depth and emotional resonance

## Design Aesthetic

Abstract expressionist style defined by vast, luminous fields of color that fill the canvas. Inspired by Rothko, Newman, and Frankenthaler, compositions rely on color relationships rather than shapes or lines to create mood. Edges between color zones are soft and atmospheric, creating a meditative, immersive visual experience.

## Background

- Color: Deep Warm Tone (#8B4513) or Saturated Hue Field (#1A237E) -- varies by composition
- Texture: Soft stained-canvas effect with subtle color gradation at zone boundaries

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Minimal thin sans-serif with generous letter-spacing. Quiet and unobtrusive so as not to compete with color. Light weight preferred, sometimes floating within a color field.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Ultra-clean sans-serif in light or regular weight. Subtle presence that recedes behind the color experience. High contrast against its background field.

## Visual Elements

- Large rectangular color zones filling most of the canvas
- Soft, feathered edges between color areas
- Floating text within color fields
- Thin horizon-like lines separating regions
- Subtle color gradation within each field
- Generous margins that become their own color space
- Vertical stripe compositions for emphasis slides
- Luminous color saturation that appears to glow

## Style Rules

### Do

- Let color be the primary communicator of mood
- Use large unbroken areas of single hues
- Create soft transitions between color zones
- Maintain extreme simplicity in layout
- Choose colors for their emotional resonance

### Don't

- Add illustrations, icons, or figurative elements
- Use sharp geometric shapes or outlines
- Include patterns, textures, or photographic elements
- Overcrowd slides with text
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Art gallery presentations, meditation and wellness content, abstract concept talks, emotional storytelling, brand mood explorations, creative direction reviews, music or sound design presentations
