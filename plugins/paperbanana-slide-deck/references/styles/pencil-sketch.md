# Pencil Sketch

Hand-drawn graphite pencil aesthetic with expressive line work and tonal shading

## Design Aesthetic

Raw and intimate, as if every visual was sketched by hand in a quality drawing notebook. Graphite strokes range from light construction lines to bold dark contours. Tonal gradients are built through hatching and cross-hatching rather than smooth fills, giving each composition an authentic hand-crafted character.

## Background

- Color: Drawing Paper (#F5F0EB)
- Texture: Visible paper tooth with subtle fiber texture

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Caveat or similar handwritten font with natural stroke variation. Bold and expressive, as if drawn with a soft pencil. Personal and approachable.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Lora or similar humanist serif. Warm and readable, complementing the hand-drawn feel without competing with sketch elements.

## Visual Elements

- Visible pencil stroke direction and pressure variation
- Cross-hatching for shadow and tonal areas
- Construction lines left partially visible
- Smudged graphite effects for soft shadows
- Clean contour lines with confident strokes
- Eraser highlights creating bright focal points
- Margin notes and annotation-style labels
- Loose gestural underdraws peeking through

## Style Rules

### Do

- Maintain visible hand-drawn quality in all elements
- Use hatching and cross-hatching for value
- Keep the palette strictly monochromatic (graphite tones)
- Allow imperfections that convey authenticity
- Use line weight variation for visual hierarchy

### Don't

- Apply color fills or gradients
- Use perfectly geometric computer-drawn shapes
- Include photographic elements
- Make lines uniformly thick or thin
- Add digital effects like glows or drop shadows

## Best For

Concept development, storyboarding, architecture sketches, creative brainstorming, art education, product ideation, sketchbook-style portfolios
