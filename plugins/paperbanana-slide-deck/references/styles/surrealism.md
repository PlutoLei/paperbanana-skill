# Surrealism

Dreamlike surrealist compositions with expressive organic forms and mysterious atmosphere

## Design Aesthetic

Channeling Dali, Magritte, and Ernst, this style embraces the irrational and dreamlike. Familiar objects appear in impossible contexts, organic forms melt and morph, and the boundary between reality and imagination dissolves. Compositions are dramatic, emotionally charged, and rich with symbolic meaning.

## Background

- Color: Twilight Gradient (#1A1A2E to #16213E)
- Texture: Smooth painterly surface with subtle atmospheric haze

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Bodoni Moda or similar high-contrast serif. Italic for dreamlike elegance. Dramatic thick-thin contrast that echoes surrealist duality.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Libre Baskerville or similar classical serif. Grounded and readable, providing stability against surreal imagery.

## Visual Elements

- Melting and morphing object forms
- Impossible spatial relationships and perspectives
- Dramatic clouds and atmospheric skies
- Organic flowing lines and biomorphic shapes
- Objects in unexpected scale relationships
- Floating and levitating elements
- Long dramatic shadows cast at odd angles
- Mirror reflections showing different realities

## Style Rules

### Do

- Create unexpected juxtapositions of familiar objects
- Use dramatic lighting with strong directional sources
- Maintain painterly quality in all visual elements
- Build compositions with symbolic meaning and narrative
- Let organic forms flow naturally and expressively

### Don't

- Use rigid geometric layouts
- Include modern UI elements or icons
- Apply flat corporate color schemes
- Make compositions literally descriptive
- Use sharp vector-style line work

## Best For

Creative writing workshops, philosophy lectures, psychology presentations, art exhibitions, imaginative storytelling, innovation brainstorming
