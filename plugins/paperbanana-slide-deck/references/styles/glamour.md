# Glamour

High-fashion luxurious style with opulent elegance, stunning beauty, and professional polish

## Design Aesthetic

Lavish and sophisticated compositions that exude high fashion and luxury. Rich textures, dramatic lighting, and refined elegance create an atmosphere of exclusive opulence. Every element is styled to perfection with meticulous attention to sensual detail and visual richness.

## Background

- Color: Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A)
- Texture: Smooth satin or velvet fabric texture with subtle sheen gradient

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Didot or similar ultra-high-contrast fashion serif. Razor-thin hairlines and bold verticals embody haute couture editorial style. Commanding and luxurious.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Josefin Sans or similar elegant thin-weight sans-serif for body text. Fashion magazine quality with refined spacing.

## Visual Elements

- Soft focus bokeh and light bloom effects
- Satin and silk fabric texture overlays
- Rose gold and metallic foil accent details
- Dramatic studio lighting with beauty dish quality
- High-fashion silhouette compositions
- Mirror and reflection effects
- Elegant script flourishes as dividers
- Gemstone and jewelry-inspired decorative elements

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use rich, warm tones with metallic accents
- Create polished, high-production-value compositions
- Apply professional beauty lighting techniques
- Maintain an atmosphere of exclusive luxury
- Use elegant, refined typographic styling

### Don't

- Include plain, modest, or understated elements
- Use distorted, grainy, or rough textures
- Create dull, low-contrast compositions
- Apply sketchy or hand-drawn aesthetics
- Use flat, digital, or utilitarian design

## Best For

Fashion brand presentations, luxury product launches, beauty industry talks, high-end event planning, celebrity portfolio showcases, premium lifestyle branding
