# Embroidery

Textile art aesthetic with intricate stitching, thread textures, and handcrafted ornamental detail

## Design Aesthetic

Every visual element appears to be rendered in embroidery thread on fabric. Intricate stitching patterns -- satin stitch, chain stitch, French knots -- build up imagery with remarkable texture and color variation. The tactile quality is paramount; you can almost feel the raised thread surfaces. Compositions blend traditional motifs (florals, fauna, folk patterns) with contemporary subjects, all unified by the thread-and-fabric medium.

## Background

- Color: Linen Natural (#EDE8DF)
- Texture: Visible woven fabric base (linen or cotton weave)

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Amatic SC or similar hand-stitched display font. Bold weight with the character of hand-embroidered lettering. Distinctive and crafted.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Josefin Sans or similar clean light sans-serif. Delicate and precise, like careful backstitch lettering.

## Visual Elements

- Visible stitch patterns (satin, chain, cross-stitch, French knots)
- Thread texture with individual strand visibility
- Fabric weave showing through unstitched areas
- Floral and botanical motifs rendered in thread
- Embroidery hoop framing for compositions
- Varied thread textures (matte, silk, metallic)
- Folk art pattern borders and decorative bands
- Raised dimensional stitching for emphasis

## Style Rules

### Do

- Make individual stitches visible and identifiable
- Show the fabric base in unstitched areas
- Use diverse stitch types for textural variety
- Include traditional embroidery motifs and patterns
- Maintain the handcrafted, tactile quality throughout

### Don't

- Use smooth digital gradients or fills
- Hide the fabric base entirely
- Apply photographic or realistic rendering
- Include modern digital UI elements
- Make stitching look uniform or machine-made

## Best For

Textile and fashion presentations, craft workshops, folk art exhibitions, cultural heritage talks, handmade brand showcases, fashion design portfolios
