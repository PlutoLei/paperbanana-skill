# Volumetric Lighting

Dramatic atmospheric lighting with visible light rays, god rays, and deep dimensional depth

## Design Aesthetic

Light itself becomes the subject. Visible beams cut through atmospheric haze, dust, or fog, creating god rays that carve three-dimensional space. The interplay of illuminated and shadowed volumes produces cinematic depth and a sense of scale. Compositions feel like moments captured in cathedrals, forests, or theatrical stages where light tells the story.

## Background

- Color: Atmospheric Dark (#111827)
- Texture: Subtle fog or haze particles visible in light beams

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Cinzel or similar elegant serif with classical proportions. Regular to semibold. Dignified and atmospheric, suited to dramatic environments.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Lato or similar clean sans-serif. Light to regular weight. Recedes gracefully in the dramatic visual environment.

## Visual Elements

- Visible light shafts cutting through space
- Atmospheric haze and fog diffusing light
- Strong directional single light sources
- Illuminated dust or particle effects
- Deep volumetric shadows creating depth layers
- Silhouettes partially revealed by light
- Light pooling on surfaces
- Atmospheric perspective fading distant elements

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use a single dominant directional light source
- Show light interacting with atmosphere (fog, dust, haze)
- Create strong depth through light and shadow layering
- Let shadows occupy significant compositional space
- Maintain warm-cool contrast between lit and shadowed areas

### Don't

- Use flat even illumination
- Add multiple competing light sources
- Make everything equally visible
- Apply clean digital-looking gradients
- Remove atmospheric effects for clarity

## Best For

Architectural presentations, cinematic storytelling, theatrical event programs, spiritual or contemplative talks, photography exhibitions, atmospheric mood boards
