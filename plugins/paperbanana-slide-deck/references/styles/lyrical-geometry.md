# Lyrical Geometry

Geometric and lyrical abstraction painting on metal with oil-paint luminosity

## Design Aesthetic

Merges the precision of geometric abstraction with the emotional expressiveness of lyrical painting. Oil on metal surfaces create a unique luminous quality where geometric forms shimmer with metallic undertones. Hard-edged shapes coexist with fluid, gestural passages. The result is contemplative, sophisticated, and deeply textured.

## Background

- Color: Brushed Metal Gray (#B8B8BA) or Dark Patina (#3A3A3E)
- Texture: Metallic surface with visible oil paint buildup and brushwork

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Refined geometric sans-serif with sharp, precise letter forms. Medium weight with clean terminals. The typography echoes the geometric precision of the artwork.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Light-weight humanist sans-serif. Elegant and readable, allowing the visual art to dominate. Subtle warmth in letterforms.

## Visual Elements

- Hard-edged geometric shapes with precise boundaries
- Fluid, lyrical brushwork passages between geometric forms
- Metallic surface sheen visible beneath translucent oil layers
- Overlapping planes with varying opacity and luminosity
- Subtle color gradients within geometric boundaries
- Textured impasto areas contrasting with smooth metallic zones
- Linear structural elements anchoring the composition
- Balanced asymmetry between order and expressive freedom

## Style Rules

### Do

- Balance geometric precision with lyrical, fluid passages
- Show visible oil paint texture and metallic surface interaction
- Use rich, luminous color with depth and transparency
- Create contemplative, sophisticated compositions
- Maintain a sense of refinement and artistic mastery

### Don't

- Use photographic or digitally rendered imagery
- Create purely rigid geometric compositions without emotion
- Apply flat, matte, texture-free color fills
- Produce chaotic or undisciplined layouts
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos
