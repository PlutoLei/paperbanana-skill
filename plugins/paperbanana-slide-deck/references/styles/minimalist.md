# Minimalist

Clean, uncluttered modern design with elegant simplicity and purposeful restraint

## Design Aesthetic

Stripped-down compositions where every element serves a clear purpose. Generous white space, clean lines, and a tightly controlled palette create visual clarity and sophistication. The beauty lies in what is removed rather than what is added.

## Background

- Color: Pure White (#FFFFFF)
- Texture: None; flat, clean surface with no noise or pattern

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Helvetica Neue Light or similar thin-weight sans-serif. Ultra-clean letterforms with perfect geometry. Understated authority through simplicity.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Inter or similar neutral sans-serif for body text. Excellent readability at all sizes. Invisible design that lets content speak.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Background | Pure White | #FFFFFF | Primary background |
| Primary Text | Soft Black | #212121 | Headlines, body text |
| Secondary Text | Medium Gray | #757575 | Captions, secondary content |
| Primary Accent | Charcoal | #333333 | Key elements, emphasis |
| Divider | Light Gray | #E0E0E0 | Rules, separators |
| Highlight | Single Accent | #000000 | Rare focal emphasis |
| Negative Space | Off White | #FAFAFA | Alternate section backgrounds |

## Visual Elements

- Generous white space as a primary design element
- Thin hairline rules for section division
- Single-weight line icons, minimal detail
- Asymmetric layouts with intentional imbalance
- Large-scale typography as visual anchor
- Monochromatic photography if imagery is used
- Precise grid alignment with mathematical spacing
- No decorative elements whatsoever

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use abundant negative space deliberately
- Limit the color palette to two or three values
- Maintain strict grid alignment
- Let typography carry the visual hierarchy
- Remove any element that does not serve content

### Don't

- Add ornamental or decorative flourishes
- Use complicated multi-color gradients
- Include cluttered layouts or dense information
- Use highly detailed illustrations
- Apply messy, noisy, or disordered compositions

## Best For

Executive briefings, design portfolios, brand strategy presentations, architectural reviews, luxury product showcases, investor updates
