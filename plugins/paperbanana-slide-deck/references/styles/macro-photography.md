# Macro Photography

Extreme close-up photographic detail revealing hidden worlds of texture, pattern, and microscopic beauty

## Design Aesthetic

Hyper-detailed photographic style that magnifies subjects to reveal extraordinary detail invisible to the naked eye. Shallow depth of field creates dramatic focus separation between razor-sharp foreground subjects and beautifully blurred backgrounds. The intimate scale transforms everyday objects into abstract landscapes of texture and color, evoking wonder and scientific curiosity.

## Background

- Color: Soft Bokeh Blur -- context-dependent (dark greens, blacks, or warm neutrals)
- Texture: Creamy bokeh (out-of-focus blur) with smooth gradient transitions

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Clean modern sans-serif with precise letter-spacing. Thin or light weight to avoid competing with photographic detail. Floating text positioned in bokeh areas for natural integration.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Neutral sans-serif in regular weight. Scientific precision without coldness. Clear and functional against both light and dark photographic backgrounds.

## Visual Elements

- Razor-sharp focal subjects against creamy bokeh
- Water droplets and dew as natural lens elements
- Extreme texture detail (insect wings, flower stamens, fabric weave)
- Shallow depth-of-field isolation effects
- Natural rim lighting and backlighting
- Reflection and refraction in tiny water surfaces
- Symmetrical patterns revealed through magnification
- Scale indicators showing actual vs. magnified size

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use dramatic shallow depth of field in every composition
- Reveal textures invisible at normal scale
- Place text in soft bokeh background areas
- Maintain photographic realism and natural lighting
- Create a sense of wonder through scale revelation

### Don't

- Show subjects at normal viewing scale
- Use flat even lighting without depth
- Apply illustration or cartoon effects
- Overcrowd compositions with multiple focal points
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Nature and wildlife education, product detail showcases, scientific research presentations, photography workshops, material science talks, food and beverage close-ups, cosmetics and skincare content
