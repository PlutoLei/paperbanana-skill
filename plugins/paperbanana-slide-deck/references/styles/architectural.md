# Architectural

Clean-lined architectural drawing style with geometric precision and modern minimalism

## Design Aesthetic

Precise and structural visual language rooted in architectural drafting and modern design. Clean lines, geometric shapes, and restrained minimalism create an atmosphere of deliberate spatial planning. Every element is placed with the intentionality of a blueprint, communicating order, clarity, and intelligent design.

## Background

- Color: Pure White (#FFFFFF) or Blueprint Pale (#F0F4F8)
- Texture: Subtle grid lines or engineering graph paper pattern

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Architectural drafting style like DIN Pro, Eurostile, or Barlow Condensed. Technical precision with modernist sensibility. Clean geometric forms.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Neutral geometric sans-serif like Helvetica Neue or Roboto. Engineered for maximum clarity and structural logic.

## Visual Elements

- Thin precise line drawings and elevations
- Grid overlays and measurement annotations
- Section cut and plan view diagrams
- Clean geometric shapes with sharp corners
- Scale bars and dimension lines
- Axonometric and isometric projections
- Minimal material texture indicators
- Technical callout labels with leader lines

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use precise thin lines for all drawn elements
- Maintain strict geometric alignment and spacing
- Include scale references and grid systems
- Keep the palette restrained and professional
- Let negative space define spatial relationships

### Don't

- Use organic curves or hand-drawn wobble
- Apply ornamental or decorative flourishes
- Create visually heavy or dense compositions
- Use bright saturated fashion colors
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Architecture firm presentations, urban planning proposals, construction project updates, interior design concepts, engineering briefings, smart building technology, spatial design reviews
