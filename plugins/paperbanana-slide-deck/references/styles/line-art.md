# Line Art

Professional line art with sleek, modern, minimalist vector graphics

## Design Aesthetic

Refined line art illustration reduced to essential contours and clean vector strokes. Every element is defined by precise, confident linework without fills, gradients, or color complexity. The style is professional, sleek, and unmistakably modern. Minimalism elevates clarity and elegance, making complex subjects approachable through pure graphic precision.

## Background

- Color: Pure White (#FFFFFF) or Very Light Gray (#F6F6F6)
- Texture: Perfectly clean and flat, no visible grain or noise

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Sleek modern sans-serif with thin to medium weight. Clean, geometric letterforms that echo the precision of the linework. Professional and contemporary.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Light-weight sans-serif matching the headline family. Minimal, readable, maintaining the refined simplicity of the overall style.

## Visual Elements

- Clean, precise vector line strokes at consistent weights
- Minimalist contour illustrations without fills
- Varying line thickness for visual hierarchy (thick outlines, thin details)
- Negative space as the primary compositional element
- Simple geometric shapes outlined, never filled
- Professional graphic icons reduced to essential strokes
- Subtle stipple or crosshatch for rare tonal areas
- Centered or grid-aligned compositions for sleek order

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use clean, confident, consistent line weights
- Maintain minimalist, uncluttered compositions
- Rely on negative space for visual breathing room
- Keep the aesthetic sleek, modern, and professional
- Use line weight variation for depth and hierarchy

### Don't

- Add photorealistic, 35mm film, or painterly effects
- Use anime, expressionist, or impressionist styles
- Create blurry, noisy, or off-center compositions
- Fill shapes with color, gradient, or texture
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Technical documentation, brand identity presentations, UX/UI design showcases, architecture and blueprint-style content, professional portfolios, instructional diagrams, clean corporate communications
