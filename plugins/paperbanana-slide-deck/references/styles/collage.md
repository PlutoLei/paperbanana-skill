# Collage

Mixed-media layered art style with textural depth and artistic juxtaposition

## Design Aesthetic

Eclectic and expressive compositions that layer torn paper, photographs, typography fragments, and painted textures into cohesive visual stories. The controlled chaos of overlapping materials creates depth and visual tension. Each element retains its own texture and origin while contributing to a unified whole.

## Background

- Color: Kraft Brown (#C4A882)
- Texture: Corrugated cardboard or kraft paper with visible fiber and torn edges

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Mix of cut-out letterforms; ransom-note style or bold slab-serif like Rockwell. Irregular sizes and angles add to the handmade collage feel.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Courier New or similar monospace typewriter font for body text. Suggests typed or stamped text pasted onto the collage surface.

## Visual Elements

- Torn and rough-cut paper edges on layered elements
- Visible tape, staples, and adhesive marks
- Overlapping materials at varied angles
- Rubber stamp and ink blot textures
- Newspaper clippings and ticket stub motifs
- Washi tape strips as section dividers
- Paint splatter and brushstroke accents
- Vintage photograph fragments with worn edges

## Style Rules

### Do

- Layer multiple textures and materials visually
- Use irregular, organic edges on elements
- Create depth through overlapping compositions
- Mix typographic styles for visual interest
- Maintain an intentional, curated messiness

### Don't

- Use clean, precise digital edges
- Apply photorealistic polished finishes
- Create symmetrical, grid-locked layouts
- Use flat, textureless color fills
- Make compositions look digitally generated

## Best For

Creative portfolio presentations, art class workshops, mood board showcases, brand identity exploration, editorial storytelling, zine-style content
