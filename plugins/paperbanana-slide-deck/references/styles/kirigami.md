# Kirigami

Japanese paper-folding and cutting art with 3D precision, symmetry, and clean architectural lines

## Design Aesthetic

Elegant three-dimensional compositions inspired by the Japanese art of cutting and folding paper. Precise geometric forms emerge from flat surfaces through careful cuts, creating intricate symmetrical structures with architectural beauty. Clean lines and mathematical precision define every element.

## Background

- Color: Rice Paper White (#F5F5F0)
- Texture: Smooth Japanese washi paper with subtle fiber texture

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Noto Sans JP or similar clean Japanese-influenced sans-serif. Precise, balanced letterforms with even stroke widths. Calm authority reflecting Japanese design philosophy.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Source Sans Pro or similar humanist sans-serif for body text. Clean and unobtrusive. Precise without being cold.

## Visual Elements

- Precise geometric cut patterns with mathematical symmetry
- 3D pop-up forms emerging from flat surfaces
- Sharp fold lines with accurate light and shadow
- Bilateral or radial symmetry in compositions
- Layered paper planes at different depths
- Clean, straight cut lines and precise angles
- Architectural structures built from paper geometry
- Negative space created by strategic cuts

## Style Rules

### Do

- Maintain mathematical precision in all geometric forms
- Use subtle shadow gradients to convey 3D depth
- Keep symmetry as a core compositional principle
- Limit the color palette to white, shadows, and one accent
- Emphasize clean lines and sharp edges

### Don't

- Use painted, drawn, or brushstroke textures
- Include noisy, blurry, or deformed elements
- Create flat 2D compositions without depth
- Use messy or organic, free-form shapes
- Apply heavy color fills that obscure paper quality

## Best For

Architecture presentations, Japanese culture showcases, design process talks, mathematical art exhibitions, paper engineering demonstrations, precision craft presentations
