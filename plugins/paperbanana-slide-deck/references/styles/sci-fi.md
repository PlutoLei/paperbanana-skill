# Sci-Fi

Futuristic science fiction style with alien worlds, space themes, and advanced civilizations

## Design Aesthetic

Epic and immersive visual language that transports viewers to advanced civilizations and alien worlds. Deep space vistas, advanced technology interfaces, and otherworldly environments create a sense of vast possibility. Cinematic scale and dramatic lighting evoke blockbuster science fiction with intellectual depth.

## Background

- Color: Deep Space Black (#050510) or Nebula Dark Blue (#0B1026)
- Texture: Star field with subtle nebula clouds or planetary surface texture

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Sci-fi display like Exo 2 Bold, Chakra Petch, or Michroma. Angular and technical with a sense of advanced engineering. Clean futuristic geometry.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Technical monospace or clean sans-serif like Source Code Pro or Barlow. Interface readability with spacecraft terminal character.

## Visual Elements

- Star field and nebula backgrounds
- Spacecraft and station silhouettes
- Holographic data displays and HUD overlays
- Planetary rings and celestial bodies
- Energy beam and warp trail effects
- Alien glyph and symbol decorations
- Advanced interface panel designs
- Terraforming and megastructure diagrams

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use deep space as the primary canvas
- Create epic sense of scale with vast environments
- Include technological interface elements
- Apply cinematic dramatic lighting
- Layer depth through atmospheric perspective

### Don't

- Use Earth-bound terrestrial settings exclusively
- Create historically grounded or period visuals
- Apply warm cozy domestic aesthetics
- Use hand-drawn or rustic styling
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Space technology presentations, astronomy and astrophysics talks, science fiction world-building, futuristic product concepts, aerospace engineering showcases, speculative design explorations, sci-fi convention panels
