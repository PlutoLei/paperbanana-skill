# Colored Pencil Art

Soft hand-drawn illustrations with visible pencil strokes, light layered color, and paper texture warmth

## Design Aesthetic

Gentle hand-crafted aesthetic that celebrates visible pencil strokes and the warmth of paper. Colors are built through delicate layering, creating soft gradients with a tactile, handmade quality. The style feels personal, approachable, and nostalgic, as if each slide were lovingly illustrated in a sketchbook.

## Background

- Color: Natural Paper White (#FAF5EF) or Warm Cream (#FFF8E7)
- Texture: Visible drawing paper grain with subtle tooth that catches pencil marks

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Hand-lettered style or rounded serif with warm personality. Slightly irregular baseline suggesting hand-drawn origin. Friendly and personal without sacrificing readability.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Soft rounded sans-serif with gentle curves. Light to medium weight to match the delicate pencil aesthetic. Warm and inviting at reading sizes.

## Visual Elements

- Visible colored pencil stroke textures on all illustrations
- Soft layered shading with visible hatching
- Hand-drawn borders and frames with slight wobble
- Sketch-style icons with pencil line quality
- Light color washes built from overlapping strokes
- Paper texture showing through translucent color layers
- Gentle vignettes fading to paper at edges
- Small hand-drawn doodles and margin annotations

## Style Rules

### Do

- Show visible pencil stroke direction and texture
- Build color through layering, not flat fills
- Maintain soft, light color values throughout
- Let paper texture show through all colored areas
- Keep line quality organic and slightly imperfect

### Don't

- Use perfectly smooth or digital-looking fills
- Apply heavy saturation or neon colors
- Create hard geometric edges
- Include photographic elements
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Children's education, storybook presentations, art class materials, personal project showcases, craft and DIY tutorials, gentle onboarding guides, nature and gardening content
