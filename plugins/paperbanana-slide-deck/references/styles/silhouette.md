# Silhouette

High-contrast minimalistic black and white style with dramatic stark shapes

## Design Aesthetic

Bold and reductive compositions where subjects are reduced to their essential outlines against contrasting backgrounds. Pure black shapes on white, or white on black, create maximum visual impact with minimal means. The drama comes from what is implied by the shape rather than revealed by detail.

## Background

- Color: Pure White (#FFFFFF)
- Texture: Clean, flat surface with no texture or grain

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Futura Bold or similar geometric sans-serif. Clean, strong shapes that complement silhouette forms. Bold and unambiguous.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Cabin or similar humanist sans-serif for body text. Warm readability while maintaining the clean graphic feel.

## Visual Elements

- Pure black silhouette shapes with crisp edges
- Dramatic figure-ground contrast
- Negative space storytelling
- Sunrise/sunset gradient backgrounds for drama
- Scene silhouettes with layered depth planes
- Profile and full-figure human silhouettes
- Tree, cityscape, and landmark outline forms
- Minimalist icon silhouettes for data points

## Style Rules

### Do

- Reduce subjects to their most recognizable outline
- Maintain maximum contrast between figure and ground
- Use negative space as an active storytelling tool
- Keep compositions dramatically simple
- Create clear, instantly readable shapes

### Don't

- Add internal detail, texture, or shading to silhouettes
- Use blurry, noisy, or low-contrast treatments
- Include color gradients within silhouette forms
- Create photorealistic or highly detailed imagery
- Add multiple competing focal points

## Best For

Inspirational keynotes, identity and branding presentations, storytelling through imagery, dramatic opening slides, motivational talks, theatre and performing arts showcases
