# Mosaic

Fragmented tile mosaic compositions assembled from colorful tesserae with rich detail

## Design Aesthetic

Imagery is constructed from small discrete fragments -- tesserae -- that together form larger pictures, patterns, and narratives. Drawing from Byzantine, Roman, and Islamic mosaic traditions, the style celebrates the beauty of assembled wholes. Each tiny piece contributes color and texture, and the visible gaps between fragments add characteristic visual rhythm.

## Background

- Color: Grout Gray (#D4D4D4) or Warm Stone (#E7DDD0)
- Texture: Visible grout lines forming a grid or organic pattern between tiles

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Philosopher or similar serif with classical Mediterranean character. Regular to bold. Dignified and culturally resonant.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Noto Sans or similar universal sans-serif. Clean and functional, ensuring readability against the busy mosaic texture.

## Visual Elements

- Individual tile shapes visible at close inspection
- Grout lines creating characteristic texture
- Curved arrangements of tiles following contours
- Color gradients achieved through tile color transitions
- Geometric border patterns framing compositions
- Opus tessellatum (regular grid) and opus vermiculatum (flowing) layouts
- Metallic gold and glass tile shimmer effects
- Fragmented edges where mosaic meets background

## Style Rules

### Do

- Make individual tiles visible as discrete elements
- Use color variation within tile fields for richness
- Include characteristic grout line patterns
- Follow classical mosaic composition principles
- Create depth through tile color gradation

### Don't

- Smooth out tile edges into continuous surfaces
- Use photographic textures for tile faces
- Eliminate visible grout lines
- Apply digital gradients within individual tiles
- Make compositions too small to see tile structure
