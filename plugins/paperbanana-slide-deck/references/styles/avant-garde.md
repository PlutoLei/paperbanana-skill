# Avant-Garde

Experimental, unconventional art style pushing boundaries with unusual compositions and bold innovation

## Design Aesthetic

Provocative and boundary-breaking compositions that challenge visual conventions. Unexpected juxtapositions, deconstructed layouts, and experimental techniques create visual experiences that are deliberately disorienting and thought-provoking. Rules exist to be questioned; tradition exists to be reimagined.

## Background

- Color: Raw White (#FCFCFC)
- Texture: Intentionally unfinished; exposed canvas, torn edges, or construction marks left visible

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Neue Machina or similar experimental display font. Unconventional letterforms that push typographic boundaries. Surprising and confrontational.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Space Grotesk or similar contemporary geometric sans-serif for body text. Modern edge while maintaining legibility. Bridges experimental and functional.

## Visual Elements

- Overlapping and colliding visual planes
- Deliberately broken grid and asymmetric layouts
- Mixed media: photography, illustration, typography as texture
- Extreme scale contrasts between elements
- Rotated, cropped, and fragmented imagery
- Visible construction marks and process artifacts
- Negative space used in unexpected ways
- Clashing styles intentionally juxtaposed

## Style Rules

### Do

- Break visual conventions deliberately and purposefully
- Use unexpected scale, rotation, and positioning
- Mix disparate visual styles within a composition
- Create visual tension through contrast and collision
- Leave evidence of the creative process visible

### Don't

- Create safe, predictable, conventional layouts
- Use blurry or noisy elements without purpose
- Apply low-contrast, passive compositions
- Follow standard grid systems rigidly
- Make deformation that reads as error rather than intent

## Best For

Contemporary art exhibitions, design school critiques, creative agency pitches, innovation and disruption talks, experimental music showcases, thought leadership on creativity
