# Stacked Papercut

Layered 3D papercut art with dimensional depth, precision-cut stacked layers, and dramatic contrast

## Design Aesthetic

Striking three-dimensional compositions where multiple layers of cut paper are stacked at varying depths. Each layer is precisely cut to reveal the layers beneath, creating a diorama-like sense of depth. High contrast between layers and dramatic shadow play between planes define the visual language.

## Background

- Color: Deep Navy (#0D253F)
- Texture: Smooth matte paper surface with subtle shadow gradients between visible layers

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Poppins Bold or similar modern geometric sans-serif. Clean, substantial letterforms that can be visually "cut out" from layers. Strong and dimensional.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Work Sans or similar clean sans-serif for body text. Modern and readable, supporting the graphic quality of the layered art.

## Visual Elements

- Multiple stacked paper layers at visible depths
- Precision-cut silhouettes revealing lower layers
- Dramatic shadow gradients between layer planes
- Landscape and scenic compositions in layered depth
- High contrast between adjacent layers
- Scalloped and organic edge cuts on layer borders
- Progressive detail from back to front layers
- Light source suggesting illumination from behind or above

## Style Rules

### Do

- Create clear visual depth through multiple layers
- Use high contrast between adjacent layer planes
- Cast realistic shadows between stacked layers
- Cut precise, clean edges on all layer silhouettes
- Build scenes with progressive foreground-to-background depth

### Don't

- Create flat, 2D compositions without layer depth
- Use blurry, noisy, or deformed shapes
- Include painted, drawn, or photographic elements
- Make single-layer designs without stacking
- Use low-contrast adjacent layers that flatten depth

## Best For

Nature and landscape presentations, scenic storytelling, shadow box art showcases, theatrical set design concepts, children's book illustration talks, dimensional design portfolios
