# Analog Film

Vintage 35mm film photography with faded colors, grain, vignette, and Kodachrome nostalgia

## Design Aesthetic

Captures the warm, imperfect beauty of analog film photography. Desaturated colors, visible grain, and soft vignetting create an authentic found-footage quality. The aesthetic channels Kodachrome warmth, Lomography experimentation, and the happy accidents of chemical development. Every image feels like a rediscovered treasure from a forgotten roll of film.

## Background

- Color: Warm Film Base (#F5EDE0) or Faded Cream (#EDE5D6)
- Texture: Visible film grain with subtle light leaks and chemical staining

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Typewriter face or vintage sans-serif with uneven ink density. Feels stamped or imprinted, not digitally crisp. Warm, nostalgic character with slight imperfections.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean vintage sans-serif or slab-serif. Readable and functional with a hint of analog warmth. Slightly softened edges.

## Visual Elements

- Visible film grain texture across all imagery
- Soft vignette darkening at image edges
- Light leak effects with warm orange or magenta bleed
- Desaturated, slightly faded color rendition
- Sprocket hole borders or film strip framing
- Chemical staining and processing artifacts
- Soft focus with slight lens imperfections
- Date stamps or frame number overlays for authenticity

## Style Rules

### Do

- Apply visible film grain uniformly across images
- Desaturate colors to feel faded and vintage
- Include subtle vignette and light leak effects
- Maintain warm, nostalgic color grading
- Create an authentic found-footage aesthetic

### Don't

- Use sharp, pristine, digitally perfect imagery
- Apply bold, saturated, or neon colors
- Create illustration, painting, or cartoon styles
- Use glitchy, deformed, or artificial distortion
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Documentary and journalism presentations, photography portfolio reviews, memoir and personal storytelling, vintage brand campaigns, music and album art discussions, travel journals, cultural nostalgia content
