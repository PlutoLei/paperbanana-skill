# HDR

High dynamic range photography style with vivid detail, rich shadows, and enhanced contrast

## Design Aesthetic

Hyper-detailed compositions where every shadow and highlight reveals extraordinary depth of information. Colors are vivid and saturated, textures are razor-sharp, and the tonal range spans from deep blacks to brilliant highlights without losing detail in either extreme. The world looks enhanced, almost surreal in its clarity.

## Background

- Color: Neutral Gray (#404040)
- Texture: Smooth with ultra-fine detail, mimicking high-resolution sensor output

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Archivo Black or similar bold grotesque sans-serif. Heavy, impactful letterforms that match the intensity of HDR imagery. Vivid and commanding.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Roboto or similar versatile sans-serif for body text. Clear and sharp at all sizes. Technical yet accessible.

## Visual Elements

- Ultra-sharp detail in all visual elements
- Full tonal range from deep shadow to bright highlight
- Enhanced color saturation in all imagery
- Dramatic sky and cloud formations
- Rich texture visibility in surfaces
- Clear shadow detail without muddy blacks
- Intense contrast without clipping
- Hyper-realistic rendering of materials and surfaces

## Style Rules

### Do

- Maximize detail visibility in both shadows and highlights
- Use vivid, rich, saturated color throughout
- Create intense contrast while preserving tonal information
- Render textures with extraordinary clarity
- Maintain a sense of enhanced reality

### Don't

- Use flat, low-contrast compositions
- Allow oversaturated or underexposed areas
- Create blurry, noisy, or soft-focus effects
- Apply overexposed blown-out highlights
- Use muted, desaturated color palettes

## Best For

Landscape and travel photography showcases, real estate presentations, automotive reveals, product detail showcases, nature and wildlife talks, architectural portfolio presentations
