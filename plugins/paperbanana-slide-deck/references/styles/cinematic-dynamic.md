# Cinematic Dynamic

Epic action movie stills with motion blur, dramatic lighting, and raw photographic intensity

## Design Aesthetic

Captures the kinetic energy of high-budget action cinema. Subjects burst with motion blur and dynamic posing against dramatically lit environments. Raw photographic quality meets cinematic color grading. Every frame feels like a freeze-frame from an explosive set piece, conveying urgency, power, and visceral impact.

## Background

- Color: Deep Cinematic Black (#0A0A0A) or Dark Charcoal (#1A1A1E)
- Texture: Film grain with subtle lens flare artifacts and bokeh highlights

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Bold extended sans-serif or cinematic title face. High impact, wide tracking, often metallic or embossed appearance. Set large against dark backgrounds for maximum drama.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Clean condensed sans-serif. Functional, modern, readable against moody backgrounds with slight glow or shadow for legibility.

## Visual Elements

- Motion blur streaks conveying speed and energy
- Dramatic directional lighting with deep shadows
- Lens flare and bokeh effects for cinematic depth
- High-contrast silhouettes against illuminated backgrounds
- Particle effects: sparks, debris, smoke trails
- Cinemascope-ratio framing with black bars
- Film grain overlay for raw photographic texture
- Dynamic diagonal compositions suggesting action

## Style Rules

### Do

- Use dramatic directional lighting and deep shadows
- Apply motion blur to convey kinetic energy
- Maintain raw, photographic quality in all imagery
- Use high contrast between light and dark areas
- Create a sense of cinematic scale and epic scope

### Don't

- Use static, flat, or evenly lit compositions
- Apply cartoon, illustration, or painted styles
- Create slow, calm, or motionless imagery
- Use low-resolution or soft-focus rendering
- Add slide numbers, footers, or logos

## Best For

Action and entertainment presentations, film and media studies, product launch reveals, sports and extreme activity content, gaming showcases, dramatic storytelling, high-energy event decks
